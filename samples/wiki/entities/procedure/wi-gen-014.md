# WI-GEN-014 Generator brush replacement

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

WI-GEN-014 is a Revision A work instruction for generator brush replacement.[^c1][^c2] It applies to [WTG-01](../asset/wtg-01.md) through [WTG-12](../asset/wtg-12.md) with a 690 V double-fed asynchronous [generator](../component/generator.md).[^c1][^c2] Maintenance tasks MP-GEN-01 and MP-GEN-02 reference WI-GEN-014.[^c3][^c4]

## Facts

- WI-GEN-014 is a Revision A work instruction applicable from 2025-09-15 to [WTG-01](../asset/wtg-01.md) through [WTG-12](../asset/wtg-12.md) with a 690 V double-fed asynchronous [generator](../component/generator.md).[^c1][^c2]
- Generator brush inspection task MP-GEN-01 is scheduled every 6 months and references WI-GEN-014.[^c3]
- [Slip ring](../component/slip-ring.md) cleaning task MP-GEN-02 is scheduled every 12 months and references WI-GEN-014.[^c4]
- WO-26-0436 for [WTG-09](../asset/wtg-09.md) replaced [generator brushes](../component/generator-brushes.md) for [A3210](../alarm-code/a3210.md), was completed under WI-GEN-014, used part [LWS-P-51207](../part/lws-p-51207.md) quantity 1, and notes the shortest brush was 16 mm.[^c5]
- For WTG-09, the shortest measured brush was B3 at 16 mm, below the 18 mm threshold in WI-GEN-014.[^c6]

## Timeline
