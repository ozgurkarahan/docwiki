# SOP-LOTO-001 Lockout/tagout of wind turbines

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

SOP-LOTO-001 is the lockout/tagout procedure for wind turbines.[^c1][^c2] It is Revision C and was effective on 2024-09-02.[^c1][^c2] Isolation follows SOP-LOTO-001 and requires a personal padlock and name tag.[^c3]

## Facts

- The turbine must be isolated according to SOP-LOTO-001 before the oil circuit is opened.[^c4][^c5]
- SOP-LOTO-001 is Revision C and was effective on 2024-09-02.[^c1][^c2]
- The [generator](../component/generator.md), including stator and rotor, must be locked out according to SOP-LOTO-001.[^c6]
- Isolation follows SOP-LOTO-001 and requires a personal padlock and name tag.[^c3]
- Superseded: Referenced documents include LWS-MM-GBX-32 Rev. D section 4.3, SOP-LOTO-001, [SOP-WAH-003](sop-wah-003.md), and oil level check at the sight glass.[^c7]
- Superseded: The work starts by obtaining the PTW, stopping the turbine, and applying lockout/tagout according to SOP-LOTO-001 with the rotor locked and drivetrain isolated.[^c8]

## Timeline

- 2024-09-02: SOP-LOTO-001 is Revision C and was effective on 2024-09-02.[^c1][^c2]
