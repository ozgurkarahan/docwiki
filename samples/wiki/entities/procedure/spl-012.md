# SPL-012 Gearbox oil level check

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

SPL-012 is titled "[Gearbox](../component/gearbox.md) oil level check."[^c1] It is used to check the oil level at the sight glass and top up only with [PWG 220](../product/pwg-220.md).[^c2]

## Facts

- After 20 minutes of standstill, the oil level is checked at the sight glass according to SPL-012 and topped up only with [PWG 220](../product/pwg-220.md).[^c2]
- SPL-012 is titled "[Gearbox](../component/gearbox.md) oil level check."[^c1]
