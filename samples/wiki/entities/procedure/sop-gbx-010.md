# SOP-GBX-010 Gearbox oil change

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

SOP-GBX-010 is a standard operating procedure for [gearbox](../component/gearbox.md) oil change.[^c1][^c2][^c3][^c4] Revision B is effective from 2025-08-25 and supersedes Revision A.[^c5][^c6] It is referenced by gearbox maintenance tasks, including the [PWG 220](../product/pwg-220.md) gearbox oil change task.[^c7][^c8]

## Facts

- For the hot-oil and spill hazard, controls include SOP-GBX-010, a spill kit, and draining between 40 and 60 °C.[^c9]
- Action 1 is to revise SOP-GBX-010 to Rev. B, owned by Dario Penwarden and due 2025-08-29.[^c10]
- SOP-GBX-010 is Revision B and is effective from 2025-08-25.[^c5]
- Revision B supersedes SOP-GBX-010 Rev. A and implements MOC-2025-012 and [SB-LWS-2025-07](../bulletin/sb-lws-2025-07.md) Rev. A.[^c6]
- [Gearbox](../component/gearbox.md) [oil filter element](../component/oil-filter.md) replacement task MP-GBX-02 uses part [FGT-P-11872](../part/fgt-p-11872.md), is scheduled every 12 months, and references SOP-GBX-010.[^c7]
- Gearbox oil change task MP-GBX-03 for [PWG 220](../product/pwg-220.md) is scheduled every 5 years or on condition, references SOP-GBX-010 Rev. B, has a duration field of 6 h, and notes that the next planned changes start from 2030.[^c8]
- Superseded: SOP-GBX-010 is a standard operating procedure, Revision A, effective 2023-04-03.[^c1][^c2][^c3][^c4]

## Timeline

- 2023-04-03: SOP-GBX-010 is a standard operating procedure, Revision A, effective 2023-04-03.[^c1][^c2][^c3][^c4]
- 2025-07: Revision B supersedes SOP-GBX-010 Rev. A and implements MOC-2025-012 and [SB-LWS-2025-07](../bulletin/sb-lws-2025-07.md) Rev. A.[^c6]
- 2025-08-25: SOP-GBX-010 is Revision B and is effective from 2025-08-25.[^c5]
- 2025-08-29: Action 1 is to revise SOP-GBX-010 to Rev. B, owned by Dario Penwarden and due 2025-08-29.[^c10]
