# SOP-WAH-003 Working at height and rescue

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

SOP-WAH-003 is the working at height and rescue procedure.[^c1][^c2] It is Revision B and is effective 2026-04-07.[^c1] All work at height must follow SOP-WAH-003.[^c2]

## Facts

- SOP-WAH-003 is Revision B and is effective 2026-04-07.[^c1]
- [Rescue kit](../component/rescue-kit.md) inspection task MP-HSE-01 is scheduled every 6 months and references SOP-WAH-003 section 7.[^c3]
- Fall arrest rail and ladder inspection task MP-HSE-02 is scheduled every 12 months and references SOP-WAH-003.[^c4]
- Corrective action A2 requires making tool tethering mandatory above 2 m and updating SOP-WAH-003; Greta Vollan owns the open action due 2026-04-15.[^c5]
- All work at height must follow SOP-WAH-003.[^c2]
- Superseded: Referenced documents include LWS-MM-GBX-32 Rev. D section 4.3, [SOP-LOTO-001](sop-loto-001.md), SOP-WAH-003, and oil level check at the sight glass.[^c6]

## Timeline
