# S420 Curtailed - environmental condition

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

S420 Curtailed - environmental condition is a status code used by [WTG-03](../asset/wtg-03.md), [WTG-04](../asset/wtg-04.md), and [WTG-05](../asset/wtg-05.md).[^c1][^c2][^c3][^c4][^c5][^c6][^c7][^c8][^c9][^c10][^c11][^c12] In the SCADA alarm and status log, these turbines were in S420 Curtailed - environmental condition from around 22:00 on 2026-06-01 to around 05:00 on 2026-06-02, and again from 22:00 on 2026-08-25 to 05:00 on 2026-08-26.[^c1][^c2][^c3][^c4][^c5][^c6][^c7][^c8][^c9][^c10][^c11][^c12]

## Facts

- [WTG-03](../asset/wtg-03.md), [WTG-04](../asset/wtg-04.md), and [WTG-05](../asset/wtg-05.md) started S420 Curtailed - environmental condition around 22:00 on 2026-06-01 and ended the curtailment around 05:00 on 2026-06-02.[^c1][^c2][^c3][^c4][^c5][^c6]
- WTG-03, WTG-04 and WTG-05 entered S420 Curtailed - environmental condition at 22:00 on 2026-08-25, and the curtailment ended at 05:00 on 2026-08-26.[^c7][^c8][^c9][^c10][^c11][^c12]
