# S310 Maintenance mode

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

S310 is the status code for Maintenance mode.[^c1][^c2] It is used in wind turbine lockout/tagout and SCADA status logs.[^c1][^c3][^c4][^c5][^c6][^c7][^c8][^c9][^c10][^c11][^c12][^c13][^c14]

## Facts

- The isolation sequence starts by informing the control room and setting the turbine to maintenance mode, status S310.[^c1]
- The last person checks that tools are removed and guards are fitted before the turbine leaves maintenance mode.[^c2]
- [WTG-09](../asset/wtg-09.md) had [A3210 Generator brush wear limit reached](../alarm-code/a3210.md) raised on 2026-07-16 and cleared on 2026-07-18; WTG-09 was also in S310 Maintenance mode on 2026-07-18 from 07:58:12 to 11:52:40.[^c3][^c4][^c5][^c6]
- [WTG-04](../asset/wtg-04.md) entered S310 Maintenance mode on 2026-08-07, raised and cleared [A1001 Emergency stop activated](../alarm-code/a1001.md) during that maintenance period, and then ended S310.[^c7][^c8][^c9][^c10]
- [WTG-02](../asset/wtg-02.md) and [WTG-05](../asset/wtg-05.md) entered S310 Maintenance mode on 2026-08-25 in the morning, and both maintenance mode intervals ended later the same day.[^c11][^c12][^c13][^c14]

- 2026-08-25: [WTG-02](../asset/wtg-02.md) and [WTG-05](../asset/wtg-05.md) entered S310 Maintenance mode on 2026-08-25 in the morning, and both maintenance mode intervals ended later the same day.[^c11][^c12][^c13][^c14]
