# S510 Stopped - fault

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

S510 Stopped - fault is a status code used in SCADA alarm and status logs.[^c1][^c2][^c3][^c4][^c5][^c6][^c7][^c8][^c9][^c10][^c11][^c12][^c13][^c14][^c15][^c16][^c17][^c18][^c19][^c20][^c21][^c22][^c23][^c24][^c25][^c26][^c27][^c28][^c29][^c30][^c31][^c32][^c33][^c34][^c35][^c36][^c37][^c38][^c39][^c40][^c41][^c42][^c43][^c44][^c45][^c46][^c47][^c48][^c49][^c50][^c51][^c52][^c53][^c54][^c55][^c56][^c57][^c58][^c59] It appears with [pitch system fault](../alarm-code/a4105.md) events, [gearbox](../component/gearbox.md) oil sump temperature alarms and trips, and [grid voltage out of range](../alarm-code/a6101.md) alarms across multiple wind turbines.[^c1][^c2][^c3][^c4][^c9][^c10][^c11][^c60][^c61][^c62][^c12][^c13][^c14][^c15][^c16][^c17][^c18][^c19][^c20][^c21][^c22][^c23][^c24][^c25][^c26][^c27][^c28][^c29][^c30][^c31][^c32][^c33][^c63][^c64][^c65][^c34][^c35][^c36][^c37][^c38][^c39][^c40][^c41][^c42][^c43][^c44][^c45][^c46][^c47][^c48][^c49][^c50][^c51][^c52][^c53][^c54][^c55][^c56][^c57][^c58][^c59] The logs show S510 starting and ending when those alarms are raised and cleared.[^c1][^c2][^c3][^c4][^c5][^c6][^c7][^c8][^c60][^c61][^c62][^c12][^c13][^c14][^c15][^c16][^c17][^c30][^c31][^c32][^c33][^c34][^c35][^c36][^c37][^c38][^c39][^c40][^c41][^c42][^c43][^c44][^c45][^c46][^c47][^c48][^c49][^c50][^c51][^c52][^c53][^c54][^c55][^c56][^c57][^c58][^c59]

## Facts

- On 2026-04-14 and 2026-04-16, [WTG-12](../asset/wtg-12.md) logged [A4105 Pitch system fault](../alarm-code/a4105.md) events with S510 Stopped - fault starts and ends; the first event had value 1 axis and the second had value 2 axis.[^c1][^c2][^c3][^c4]
- On 2026-04-20, [A6101 Grid voltage out of range](../alarm-code/a6101.md) cleared at 99.6 %, with corresponding S510 Stopped - fault END entries recorded between 20:49:12 and 20:55:06.[^c5][^c6][^c7][^c8]
- On 2026-05-30, [WTG-07](../asset/wtg-07.md) raised [A2101 Gearbox oil sump temperature high](../alarm-code/a2101.md) at 75.3 °C, then raised [A2102 Gearbox oil sump temperature trip](../alarm-code/a2102.md) at 82.6 °C and entered S510 Stopped - fault.[^c9][^c10][^c11]
- WTG-07 cleared the 2026-05-30 A2102 trip at 79.4 °C, cleared the A2101 high-temperature alarm at 72.8 °C, and ended S510 Stopped - fault.[^c60][^c61][^c62]
- On 2026-06-06, A6101 Grid voltage out of range alarms were raised across [WTG-01](../asset/wtg-01.md) through WTG-12 with value 87.2 %, accompanied by S510 Stopped - fault START entries; later A6101 clear entries show value 99.6 % and S510 END entries.[^c12][^c13][^c14][^c15][^c16][^c17]
- On 2026-06-18 at about 14:10, A6101 Grid voltage out of range alarms were raised on WTG-01 through WTG-12 at 89.2 %, with S510 Stopped - fault status starts recorded for each turbine in the same sequence.[^c18][^c19][^c20][^c21][^c22][^c23][^c24][^c25][^c26][^c27][^c28][^c29]
- The 2026-06-18 grid-voltage alarms cleared at 99.6 %, and the S510 Stopped - fault statuses ended between [WTG-06](../asset/wtg-06.md) at 14:15:04 and WTG-12 at 14:20:10.[^c30][^c31][^c32][^c33]
- On 2026-06-19, WTG-07 first raised A2101 Gearbox oil sump temperature high at 75.6 °C, then raised A2102 Gearbox oil sump temperature trip at 83.4 °C and entered S510 Stopped - fault.[^c63][^c64][^c65]
- On 2026-08-02, A6101 Grid voltage out of range was raised for WTG-01 through WTG-12 with value 89.3 %, S510 Stopped - fault started, then the A6101 alarms cleared at 99.6 % and S510 ended.[^c34][^c35][^c36][^c37][^c38][^c39]
- [WTG-05](../asset/wtg-05.md) raised A4105 Pitch system fault with value 1 axis on 2026-08-03, started S510 Stopped - fault, then cleared A4105 and ended S510 about two hours later.[^c40][^c41][^c42][^c43]
- WTG-06 raised A4105 Pitch system fault with value 3 axis on 2026-08-05, started S510 Stopped - fault, then cleared A4105 and ended S510 shortly after midnight on 2026-08-06.[^c44][^c45][^c46][^c47]
- On 2026-08-21, WTG-01 through WTG-12 raised A6101 Grid voltage out of range at 87.8 %, entered S510 Stopped - fault, then cleared A6101 at 99.6 % and ended S510.[^c48][^c49][^c50][^c51][^c52][^c53][^c54][^c55]
- [WTG-10](../asset/wtg-10.md) raised A4105 Pitch system fault with value 2 axis on 2026-08-23 01:43:03, entered S510 Stopped - fault, cleared A4105 at 04:03:03, and ended S510 at 04:03:33.[^c56][^c57][^c58][^c59]
