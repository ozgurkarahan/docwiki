# S200 Ready - low wind

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

S200 Ready - low wind is a status code used for [WTG-01](../asset/wtg-01.md) through [WTG-12](../asset/wtg-12.md).[^c1][^c2][^c3][^c4][^c5][^c6][^c7][^c8][^c9][^c10][^c11][^c12] The status was recorded on 2026-04-10, 2026-07-24, and 2026-08-23.[^c1][^c2][^c3][^c4][^c5][^c6][^c7][^c8][^c9][^c10][^c11][^c12] It started when wind speed was low and ended later at higher wind speeds.[^c5][^c6][^c7][^c8][^c9][^c10][^c11][^c12]

## Facts

- On 2026-04-10, S200 Ready - low wind started for [WTG-01](../asset/wtg-01.md) through [WTG-12](../asset/wtg-12.md) between 11:00:11 and 11:01:28 and ended between 16:00:13 and 16:01:30.[^c1][^c2][^c3][^c4]
- On 2026-07-24, WTG-01 through WTG-12 entered S200 Ready - low wind status around 07:00 at 2.2 wind_speed_ms and ended S200 around 12:00 at 3.6 wind_speed_ms.[^c5][^c6][^c7][^c8]
- On 2026-08-23, WTG-01 through WTG-12 started S200 Ready - low wind at wind speed 2.4 m/s and later ended S200 at wind speed 3.3 m/s.[^c9][^c10][^c11][^c12]
