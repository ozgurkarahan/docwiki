# Litware Wind Systems

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

Litware Wind Systems is an organisation that issues service bulletins and maintenance documents for [Litware LT-32](../product/lt-32.md) CCV turbines.[^c1][^c2][^c3] At [Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm](../asset/quellmoor-ridge-wind-farm.md), its LT-32 turbines are recorded as [WTG-01](../asset/wtg-01.md) to [WTG-12](../asset/wtg-12.md) and are associated with [Fabrikam FGT-3200](../product/fgt-3200.md) gearboxes.[^c4][^c5][^c6][^c7][^c8][^c9][^c10][^c11][^c12][^c13][^c14][^c15][^c16][^c17][^c18] Its instructions and forms are returned to Litware Wind Systems for reporting or completion after certain turbine work.[^c19][^c2][^c20]

## Facts

- Litware Wind Systems issued the service bulletin on 2025-03-14.[^c1]
- The completion form in annex B must be returned to Litware Wind Systems within 30 days of each conversion.[^c19]
- Litware Wind Systems issued the service bulletin on 2025-10-06.[^c2]
- Every bolt that turns more than 60 degrees before reaching the torque must be recorded and reported to Litware Wind Systems.[^c2]
- The turbine, blade, torque wrench serial number, and bolts that turned must be recorded on the re-torque form, which must be returned to Litware Wind Systems within 60 days.[^c2]
- The excerpt is Chapter 4, [Gearbox](../component/gearbox.md), of document LWS-MM-GBX-32 Rev. D, issued by Litware Wind Systems on 2024-03-15.[^c3]
- Litware Wind Systems publishes changes to the gearbox chapter as service bulletins, and the bulletin register must be checked before planning gearbox work.[^c3]
- Litware support confirmed on 2025-05-20 under ticket LWS-TS-48213 that [SB-LWS-2025-07](../bulletin/sb-lws-2025-07.md) applies to all 12 CCV turbines of [Quellmoor Ridge](../asset/quellmoor-ridge-wind-farm.md).[^c21]
- Action 6 is to send the Litware completion form within 30 days of each conversion, owned by Dario Penwarden and due per conversion.[^c22]
- The report covers Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm, which is recorded as having 12 [Litware LT-32](../product/lt-32.md) turbines and 36 blades of 57.5 m.[^c4]
- The new blade is lifted, bolted, and torqued according to a Litware instruction.[^c23]
- The procedure applies to [WTG-01](../asset/wtg-01.md) to [WTG-12](../asset/wtg-12.md) with Litware LT-32 CCV turbines and [Fabrikam FGT-3200](../product/fgt-3200.md) gearboxes.[^c5]
- Quantities and batch numbers are recorded in the work order, and the completion form is sent to Litware Wind Systems within 30 days.[^c20]
- The instruction describes checking and replacing the [generator brushes](../component/generator-brushes.md) of Litware LT-32 wind turbines at the Quellmoor Ridge wind farm.[^c24]
- A request to Litware Wind Systems to add fan motor current to SCADA signals is open under ticket LWS-TS-51877.[^c25]
- All turbine entries are Litware LT-32 CCV turbines with Fabrikam FGT-3200 gearboxes, Hub height (m) 92, and Rotor diameter (m) 118.[^c6][^c7][^c8][^c9][^c10][^c11][^c12][^c13][^c14][^c15][^c16][^c17]
- Supplier typical lead times are 10 weeks for [Fabrikam Getriebetechnik GmbH](fabrikam-getriebetechnik.md), 6 weeks for Litware Wind Systems, 3 weeks for [Proseware Lubricants](proseware-lubricants.md), and 2 weeks for [Adventure Works Industrial Supply](adventure-works-industrial-supply.md).[^c26][^c27][^c28][^c29]
- The deck is about the Fabrikam FGT-3200 in the Litware LT-32 and is dated October 2025.[^c30]
- A new blade was ordered from Litware Wind Systems for delivery at the end of August.[^c31]
- Quellmoor Ridge has 12 Litware LT-32 turbines in CCV configuration, identified WTG-01 to WTG-12, with total capacity of 38.4 MW.[^c18]
- Litware’s response addresses a 29 April 2025 question about SB-LWS-2025-07 Rev. A, described as covering gearbox oil viscosity grade for CCV turbines.[^c32]
- The [service lift](../component/service-lift.md) on [WTG-06](../asset/wtg-06.md) stopped twice the previous winter because of cold, and such events should be reported every time for follow-up with Litware.[^c33][^c34]
- Superseded: The procedure applies to WTG-01 to WTG-12, specified as Litware LT-32 CCV turbines with Fabrikam FGT-3200 gearboxes.[^c35]
