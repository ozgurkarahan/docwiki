# Tailspin Heavy Lift

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

Tailspin Heavy Lift is an organisation involved in planning a crane lift for the [WTG-04](../asset/wtg-04.md) blade replacement.[^c1][^c2] Bram Okkerse of Tailspin Heavy Lift is the appointed person for this lift plan.[^c1]

## Facts

- Bram Okkerse of Tailspin Heavy Lift is the appointed person, and Dario Penwarden of [Contoso Renewables](contoso-renewables.md) is the client supervisor.[^c1]
- The crane lift for the [WTG-04](../asset/wtg-04.md) blade replacement was to be planned with Tailspin Heavy Lift for early September.[^c2]
