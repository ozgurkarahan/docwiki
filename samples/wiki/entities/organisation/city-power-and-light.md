# City Power & Light

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

City Power & Light is a utility organisation connected to the Skelvane 132 kV substation and the [Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm](../asset/quellmoor-ridge-wind-farm.md) site.[^c1][^c2][^c3] Its Network Control issues curtailment instructions for planned network outages, including CPL-CI-2026-018.[^c4] City Power & Light also announced July network works on the Skelvane 132 kV circuits.[^c5]

## Facts

- The document is a City Power & Light Network Control curtailment instruction for a planned network outage, numbered CPL-CI-2026-018.[^c4]
- The Network Control duty engineer at City Power & Light is available 24/7.[^c4]
- The [Quellmoor Ridge substation](../asset/quellmoor-ridge-substation.md) is a 33/132 kV substation with one 45 MVA transformer, located next to the O&M building, with connection to City Power & Light, Skelvane 132 kV substation, circuit 2.[^c1]
- City Power & Light announced July network works on the Skelvane 132 kV circuits, with the formal curtailment instruction expected in early July.[^c5]
- The site connects to City Power & Light at the Skelvane 132 kV substation.[^c2]
- A 132 kV line is labelled as going to Skelvane substation, with City Power & Light named in the label.[^c3]
