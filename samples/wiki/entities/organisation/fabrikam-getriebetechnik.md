# Fabrikam Getriebetechnik GmbH

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

Fabrikam Getriebetechnik GmbH is a supplier of [FGT-3200](../product/fgt-3200.md) gearboxes.[^c1][^c2][^c3][^c4][^c5] Its supplier lead time was listed as 10 weeks.[^c1][^c2][^c3][^c4] Fabrikam confirmed delivery of two HSS bearings and offered air freight for an extra EUR 2,300.[^c6][^c7]

## Facts

- The bulletin applies to [LT-32](../product/lt-32.md) turbines delivered in CCV configuration with [Fabrikam FGT-3200](../product/fgt-3200.md) gearboxes and to LT-32 standard turbines at sites where ambient temperature regularly falls below -20 °C.[^c8]
- The document applies to LT-32 turbines of all variants with the Fabrikam FGT-3200 [gearbox](../component/gearbox.md).[^c9]
- The LT-32 uses a Fabrikam FGT-3200 three-stage gearbox with one [planetary stage](../component/planetary-stage.md) followed by two parallel helical stages.[^c5]
- The asset is the [WTG-07](../asset/wtg-07.md) main gearbox, a Fabrikam FGT-3200 with serial FGT-3200-18-07752.[^c10]
- The procedure applies to [WTG-01](../asset/wtg-01.md) to [WTG-12](../asset/wtg-12.md) with Litware LT-32 CCV turbines and Fabrikam FGT-3200 gearboxes.[^c11]
- All turbine entries are Litware LT-32 CCV turbines with Fabrikam FGT-3200 gearboxes, Hub height (m) 92, and Rotor diameter (m) 118.[^c12][^c13][^c14][^c15][^c16][^c17][^c18][^c19][^c20][^c21][^c22][^c23]
- Supplier typical lead times are 10 weeks for Fabrikam Getriebetechnik GmbH, 6 weeks for [Litware Wind Systems](litware-wind-systems.md), 3 weeks for [Proseware Lubricants](proseware-lubricants.md), and 2 weeks for [Adventure Works Industrial Supply](adventure-works-industrial-supply.md).[^c1][^c2][^c3][^c4]
- The deck is about the Fabrikam FGT-3200 in the Litware LT-32 and is dated October 2025.[^c24]
- The configuration records checked for [Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm](../asset/quellmoor-ridge-wind-farm.md) showed that all 12 turbines, WTG-01 through WTG-12, were delivered as LT-32 in CCV configuration with Fabrikam FGT-3200 gearboxes, so [SB-LWS-2025-07](../bulletin/sb-lws-2025-07.md) applies to all 12 turbines.[^c25]
- For [HSS bearing](../component/hss-bearing.md) [FGT-P-22328](../part/fgt-p-22328.md), stock was 0 on hand, minimum 1, and 2 on order under PO-76994 to Fabrikam.[^c26]
- The last HSS bearing was used on [WTG-10](../asset/wtg-10.md) in March under WO-26-0187, and Fabrikam confirmed delivery of two bearings in week 39, from 21 to 25 September 2026.[^c6]
- Fabrikam offered air freight for an extra EUR 2,300, which would bring the bearings forward by about two weeks, and Tomasz asked whether to accept it.[^c7]
- Superseded: The procedure applies to WTG-01 to WTG-12, specified as Litware LT-32 CCV turbines with Fabrikam FGT-3200 gearboxes.[^c27]
