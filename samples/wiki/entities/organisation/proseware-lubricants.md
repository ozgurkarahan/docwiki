# Proseware Lubricants

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

Proseware Lubricants supplies [Proseware PWG 320](../product/pwg-320.md) and [Proseware PWG 220](../product/pwg-220.md) gear oil.[^c1][^c2][^c3][^c4][^c5][^c6][^c7][^c8][^c9] Proseware PWG 320 is an approved ISO VG 320 synthetic PAO gear oil, and Proseware PWG 220 is an ISO VG 220 gear oil used in the gearboxes of all 12 turbines.[^c1][^c2][^c9]

## Facts

- For affected turbines, the gear oil changes from [Proseware PWG 320](../product/pwg-320.md) to [Proseware PWG 220](../product/pwg-220.md).[^c10]
- Per turbine, parts and labour include 470 L of Proseware PWG 220, one [FGT-P-11872](../part/fgt-p-11872.md) [oil filter element](../component/oil-filter.md), one PWG 220 label kit, and labour by 2 technicians for about 6 hours.[^c11]
- The approved gear oil is Proseware PWG 320 with viscosity grade ISO VG 320 and synthetic PAO base oil.[^c1]
- The change replaces Proseware PWG 320 (ISO VG 320) with Proseware PWG 220 (ISO VG 220) in the gearboxes of all 12 turbines, as required by [SB-LWS-2025-07](../bulletin/sb-lws-2025-07.md) Rev. A.[^c2]
- The lubricant is Proseware PWG 220, ISO VG 220, in service since 2025-10-15.[^c3]
- Each turbine requires 470 L of Proseware PWG 220, consisting of 60 L for flushing and 410 L for filling, supplied as 3 drums of [PWS-220-D208](../part/pws-220-d208.md).[^c12]
- The [gearbox](../component/gearbox.md) is filled with 410 L of Proseware PWG 220 through a 10 micron filter cart.[^c13]
- [PWS-320-D208](../part/pws-320-d208.md) is listed as Proseware PWG 320 gear oil in a 208 L drum with a do-not-use note tied to MOC-2025-012; the stock entry shows 1 drum on hand, minimum 0, reorder quantity 0, and 0 on order.[^c4]
- Supplier typical lead times are 10 weeks for [Fabrikam Getriebetechnik GmbH](fabrikam-getriebetechnik.md), 6 weeks for [Litware Wind Systems](litware-wind-systems.md), 3 weeks for Proseware Lubricants, and 2 weeks for [Adventure Works Industrial Supply](adventure-works-industrial-supply.md).[^c5][^c6][^c7][^c8]
- Since SB-LWS-2025-07, the gearboxes use Proseware PWG 220 with ISO VG 220 instead of PWG 320.[^c9]
- The quiz answers state that the HSS drives the [generator](../component/generator.md), [A2102](../alarm-code/a2102.md) trips at 82 °C, the oil used is Proseware PWG 220, and a rising PQ index means more ferrous wear debris, often from a bearing.[^c14]
- For the conversion, the oil is to be changed from Proseware PWG 320 to Proseware PWG 220 at the next scheduled oil change, and no later than 31 December 2025.[^c15]
- Superseded: The specified new oil is Proseware PWG 320 (ISO VG 320), with 410 L per turbine supplied as 2 drums PWS-320-D208.[^c16]
- Superseded: 410 L of Proseware PWG 320 is filled through a 10 micron filter cart.[^c17]

## Timeline

- 2025-07: Since [SB-LWS-2025-07](../bulletin/sb-lws-2025-07.md), the gearboxes use [Proseware PWG 220](../product/pwg-220.md) with ISO VG 220 instead of [PWG 320](../product/pwg-320.md).[^c9]
- 2025-07: The change replaces Proseware PWG 320 (ISO VG 320) with Proseware PWG 220 (ISO VG 220) in the gearboxes of all 12 turbines, as required by SB-LWS-2025-07 Rev. A.[^c2]
- 2025-10-15: The lubricant is Proseware PWG 220, ISO VG 220, in service since 2025-10-15.[^c3]
- 2025-12-31: For the conversion, the oil is to be changed from Proseware PWG 320 to Proseware PWG 220 at the next scheduled oil change, and no later than 31 December 2025.[^c15]
