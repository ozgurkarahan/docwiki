# Skelvane District Environmental Authority

The Skelvane District Environmental Authority is the issuer of Environmental Permit EP-2018-0417 for [Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm](../asset/quellmoor-ridge-wind-farm.md).[^c1] The permit is signed for the Authority by Siobhan Carrick, Permitting Officer, and the Authority contact is the Permitting Officer for the Skelvane district.[^c2]

- The permit was issued by the Skelvane District Environmental Authority.[^c1]
