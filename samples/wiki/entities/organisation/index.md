# Organisations

Organisations by role: operator, manufacturer, suppliers, contractors, regulator and grid operator.

- [Adventure Works Industrial Supply](adventure-works-industrial-supply.md): Adventure Works Industrial Supply is a supplier with a typical lead time of 2 weeks.
- [City Power & Light](city-power-and-light.md): City Power & Light is a utility organisation connected to the Skelvane 132 kV substation and the Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm site. - [Contoso Renewables](contoso-renewables.md): Contoso Renewables is the permit holder for Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm. It is also identified as the customer or owner on several current Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm documents, including maintenance, asset, stock, and safety records. The company is described as operating 12 LT-32 turbines at…
- [Fabrikam Getriebetechnik GmbH](fabrikam-getriebetechnik.md): Fabrikam Getriebetechnik GmbH is a supplier of FGT-3200 gearboxes. Its supplier lead time was listed as 10 weeks. Fabrikam confirmed delivery of two HSS bearings and offered air freight for an extra EUR 2,300.
- [Litware Wind Systems](litware-wind-systems.md): Litware Wind Systems is an organisation that issues service bulletins and maintenance documents for Litware LT-32 CCV turbines. At Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm, its LT-32 turbines are recorded as WTG-01 to WTG-12 and are associated with Fabrikam FGT-3200 gearboxes. Its instructions and forms are…
- [Proseware Lubricants](proseware-lubricants.md): Proseware Lubricants supplies Proseware PWG 320 and Proseware PWG 220 gear oil. Proseware PWG 320 is an approved ISO VG 320 synthetic PAO gear oil, and Proseware PWG 220 is an ISO VG 220 gear oil used in the gearboxes of all 12 turbines.
- [Skelvane District Environmental Authority](skelvane-district-environmental-authority.md): The Skelvane District Environmental Authority is the issuer of Environmental Permit EP-2018-0417 for Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm. The permit is signed for the Authority by Siobhan Carrick, Permitting Officer, and the Authority contact is the Permitting Officer for the Skelvane district.
- [Tailspin Heavy Lift](tailspin-heavy-lift.md): Tailspin Heavy Lift is an organisation involved in planning a crane lift for the WTG-04 blade replacement. Bram Okkerse of Tailspin Heavy Lift is the appointed person for this lift plan.
- [Trey Research Laboratories](trey-research-laboratories.md): Trey Research Laboratories is an organisation approved the report TR-OA-2026-0614 for the WTG-07 gearbox oil sample.
- [Wingtip Blade Services](wingtip-blade-services.md): Wingtip Blade Services is a contractor for the blade drone inspection task MP-BLD-01 at Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm. The task is scheduled every 12 months.
