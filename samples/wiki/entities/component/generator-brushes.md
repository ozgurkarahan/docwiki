# generator brushes

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

Generator brushes are a component used in [Litware LT-32](../product/lt-32.md) wind turbines at the [Quellmoor Ridge wind farm](../asset/quellmoor-ridge-wind-farm.md).[^c1] Work order WO-26-0436 replaced the generator brushes on [WTG-09](../asset/wtg-09.md) under [WI-GEN-014](../procedure/wi-gen-014.md) after alarm [A3210](../alarm-code/a3210.md).[^c2][^c3]

## Facts

- The instruction describes checking and replacing the generator brushes of [Litware LT-32](../product/lt-32.md) wind turbines at the [Quellmoor Ridge wind farm](../asset/quellmoor-ridge-wind-farm.md).[^c1]
- WO-26-0436 for [WTG-09](../asset/wtg-09.md) replaced generator brushes for [A3210](../alarm-code/a3210.md), was completed under [WI-GEN-014](../procedure/wi-gen-014.md), used part [LWS-P-51207](../part/lws-p-51207.md) quantity 1, and notes the shortest brush was 16 mm.[^c2]
- WTG-09 had its generator brushes replaced under work order WO-26-0436 following alarm A3210 from 16/07.[^c3]
