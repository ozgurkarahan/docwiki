# slip ring

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

A slip ring is a component that is cleaned with kit [LWS-P-55031](../part/lws-p-55031.md) and then fitted with new brushes that are checked for free sliding.[^c1][^c2][^c3] Its cleaning task MP-GEN-02 is scheduled every 12 months and references [WI-GEN-014](../procedure/wi-gen-014.md).[^c4]

## Facts

- The slip-ring cleaning kit required is [LWS-P-55031](../part/lws-p-55031.md).[^c1]
- The slip ring must be cleaned with kit LWS-P-55031, then new brushes must be installed and checked for free sliding.[^c2][^c3]
- Slip ring cleaning task MP-GEN-02 is scheduled every 12 months and references [WI-GEN-014](../procedure/wi-gen-014.md).[^c4]
