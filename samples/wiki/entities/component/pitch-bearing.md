# pitch bearing

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

Pitch bearing bolt re-torque applies to all variants of [LT-32](../product/lt-32.md) turbines with M30 10.9 [pitch bearing bolts](pitch-bearing-bolts.md).[^c1] The task covers 100% of pitch bearing bolts and specifies a torque of 1,450 Nm in the cross pattern of the bolt map, blade by blade.[^c1][^c2] On [Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm](../asset/quellmoor-ridge-wind-farm.md), the maintenance plan schedules this task every 12 months, and work orders show it completed for [WTG-01](../asset/wtg-01.md) through [WTG-06](../asset/wtg-06.md) and planned for [WTG-07](../asset/wtg-07.md) through [WTG-12](../asset/wtg-12.md).[^c2][^c3][^c4][^c5][^c6][^c7][^c8][^c9][^c10][^c11][^c12][^c13][^c14]

## Facts

- The bulletin applies to all variants of [LT-32](../product/lt-32.md) turbines with M30 10.9 [pitch bearing bolts](pitch-bearing-bolts.md), 72 bolts per blade bearing, and 216 bolts per turbine.[^c1]
- The reason for the bulletin is that loose pitch bearing bolts were found on two LT-32 turbines at another site after three years of operation, and a one-time re-torque restores the preload.[^c1]
- Technicians must re-torque 100 % of the pitch bearing bolts to 1,450 Nm in the cross pattern of the bolt map, blade by blade.[^c1]
- Pitch bearing bolt re-torque task MP-PIT-01 covers 100 % of bolts at 1,450 Nm, is scheduled every 12 months, and references [SB-LWS-2025-11](../bulletin/sb-lws-2025-11.md) Rev. A.[^c2]
- Pitch bearing bolt re-torque work for [WTG-01](../asset/wtg-01.md) through [WTG-06](../asset/wtg-06.md) was completed with a stated torque of 1,450 Nm and coverage of 100 % of bolts, referenced to SB-LWS-2025-11 Rev. A.[^c3][^c4][^c5][^c6][^c7][^c8]
- Pitch bearing bolt re-torque work for [WTG-07](../asset/wtg-07.md) through [WTG-12](../asset/wtg-12.md) is planned for September 2026, with each title stating 1,450 Nm and 100 % of bolts and reference SB-LWS-2025-11 Rev. A.[^c9][^c10][^c11][^c12][^c13][^c14]
