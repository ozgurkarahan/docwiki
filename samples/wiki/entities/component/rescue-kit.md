# rescue kit

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

A rescue kit is kept in every nacelle and has a sealed kit with a descent device.[^c1][^c2] Its seal must be checked every day, and a broken seal means the kit must be inspected before the next climb.[^c1][^c2] Rescue kit inspections are scheduled every 6 months under MP-HSE-01.[^c3]

## Facts

- Each nacelle has a sealed rescue kit with a descent device.[^c1]
- A broken rescue kit seal means the kit must be inspected before the next climb.[^c1]
- Finding F1 was a major finding: the rescue kit in the [WTG-06](../asset/wtg-06.md) nacelle was past its 6-monthly inspection, with last inspection on 2025-06-12 and due date 2025-12-12; the action was to inspect all rescue kits and add the kit seal check to the daily checklist, owned by Greta Vollan and due 2026-03-31.[^c4]
- Rescue kit inspection task MP-HSE-01 is scheduled every 6 months and references [SOP-WAH-003](../procedure/sop-wah-003.md) section 7.[^c3]
- Near the end of the export, WO-26-0444 for [WTG-05](../asset/wtg-05.md) is in progress for rescue kit inspection, WO-26-0450 for [WTG-11](../asset/wtg-11.md) is waiting for parts for a leaking hydraulic hose in the yaw brake circuit with part [AWI-10442](../part/awi-10442.md) quantity 1, and WO-26-0457 for [WTG-03](../asset/wtg-03.md) is open for yaw brake pad inspection.[^c5][^c6][^c7]
- A sealed rescue kit is kept in every nacelle and its seal must be checked every day.[^c2]
- WTG-05 rescue kit inspection WO-26-0444 has started and is to finish next week.[^c8]
- Rescue kit inspections were performed on [WTG-01](../asset/wtg-01.md) to [WTG-04](../asset/wtg-04.md) under MP-HSE-01.[^c9]

## Timeline
