# pitch bearing bolts

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

Pitch bearing bolts are M30 10.9 bolts used on all variants of [LT-32](../product/lt-32.md) turbines, with 72 bolts per blade bearing and 216 bolts per turbine.[^c1] A current bulletin for them requires re-torquing 100% of the bolts to 1,450 Nm in the cross pattern of the bolt map, blade by blade.[^c1]

## Facts

- The bulletin applies to all variants of [LT-32](../product/lt-32.md) turbines with M30 10.9 pitch bearing bolts, 72 bolts per blade bearing, and 216 bolts per turbine.[^c1]
- The reason for the bulletin is that loose pitch bearing bolts were found on two LT-32 turbines at another site after three years of operation, and a one-time re-torque restores the preload.[^c1]
- Technicians must re-torque 100 % of the pitch bearing bolts to 1,450 Nm in the cross pattern of the bolt map, blade by blade.[^c1]
