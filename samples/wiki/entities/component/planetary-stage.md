# planetary stage

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

A planetary stage is a [gearbox](gearbox.md) stage with a planet carrier, three planet gears, a ring gear, and a sun pinion.[^c1] The [LT-32](../product/lt-32.md) uses a three-stage gearbox with one planetary stage followed by two parallel helical stages.[^c2]

## Facts

- The [LT-32](../product/lt-32.md) uses a [Fabrikam FGT-3200](../product/fgt-3200.md) three-stage [gearbox](gearbox.md) with one planetary stage followed by two parallel helical stages.[^c2]
- The gearbox stages described are a planetary stage with a planet carrier, three planet gears, a ring gear and a sun pinion, followed by two parallel helical stages.[^c1]
