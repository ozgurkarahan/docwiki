# oil cooler fan motor

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

The oil cooler fan motor is a component identified by part number [LWS-P-30418](../part/lws-p-30418.md).[^c1][^c2][^c3][^c4] It is a 1.5 kW critical spare part with stock below the minimum level.[^c1][^c2][^c5] For [WTG-07](../asset/wtg-07.md), the motor was replaced under WO-26-0398 and the [oil cooler](oil-cooler.md) radiator was cleaned under WO-26-0399.[^c6][^c7][^c3][^c4]

## Facts

- Corrective actions completed on 2026-06-24 included replacing oil cooler fan motor [LWS-P-30418](../part/lws-p-30418.md) under WO-26-0398 and cleaning the [oil cooler](oil-cooler.md) radiator under WO-26-0399.[^c6][^c7]
- LWS-P-30418, the 1.5 kW oil cooler fan motor, is a critical item below minimum stock: 1 pc on hand, minimum 2 pc, reorder quantity 2 pc, and 2 pc on order under PO-77102 for expected delivery on 2026-08-14.[^c1][^c2]
- For [WTG-07](../asset/wtg-07.md) under INC-2026-006, WO-26-0398 replaced the oil cooler fan motor with part LWS-P-30418 quantity 1, and WO-26-0399 cleaned the oil cooler radiator with remarks that fins were about 40 % blocked by insects and pollen.[^c3][^c4]
- Oil cooler fan motor LWS-P-30418 is also below minimum after the WTG-07 repair, with 1 on hand and a minimum of 2.[^c5]

## Timeline
