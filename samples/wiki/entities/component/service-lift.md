# service lift

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

The service lift is a component used on the tower, and climbers should use it when available.[^c1] In cold weather, it should be used when it works, and its battery should be checked on cold mornings.[^c2] One service lift stopped twice the previous winter because of cold, and those events should be reported for follow-up.[^c3][^c4]

## Facts

- Climbers must use the fall arrest rail and rest platforms, and use the service lift when available.[^c1]
- Ladder rungs can be frozen at the tower base; the service lift should be used when it works, and the lift battery should be checked on cold mornings.[^c2]
- The service lift on [WTG-06](../asset/wtg-06.md) stopped twice the previous winter because of cold, and such events should be reported every time for follow-up with [Litware](../organisation/litware-wind-systems.md).[^c3][^c4]
