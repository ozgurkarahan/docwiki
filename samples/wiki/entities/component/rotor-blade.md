# rotor blade

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

A rotor blade is a component used in the [WTG-04](../asset/wtg-04.md) Blade B replacement job, which is described as a single blade lift.[^c1] WO-26-0355 is a planned high-priority corrective-maintenance work order for WTG-04 to replace rotor blade B because of a trailing-edge crack.[^c2]

## Facts

- The job is the [WTG-04](../asset/wtg-04.md) rotor blade B replacement, described as a single blade lift.[^c1]
- WO-26-0355 for WTG-04 is a planned high-priority corrective-maintenance work order to replace rotor blade B for a trailing-edge crack, with planned date 2026-09-08 and references WBS-2026-031 and LP-2026-009.[^c2]

## Timeline

- 2026-09-08: WO-26-0355 for [WTG-04](../asset/wtg-04.md) is a planned high-priority corrective-maintenance work order to replace rotor blade B for a trailing-edge crack, with planned date 2026-09-08 and references WBS-2026-031 and LP-2026-009.[^c2]
