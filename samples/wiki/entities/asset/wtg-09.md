# WTG-09

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

WTG-09 is a wind turbine at [Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm](quellmoor-ridge-wind-farm.md).[^c1][^c2][^c3][^c4][^c5][^c6][^c7][^c8][^c9][^c10][^c11][^c12] Its [gearbox](../component/gearbox.md) oil conversion to [PWG 220](../product/pwg-220.md) is pending under [SB-LWS-2025-07](../bulletin/sb-lws-2025-07.md), and the completed gearbox oil conversion work order WO-25-0309 is recorded separately.[^c13][^c14] In 2026, WTG-09 had a [generator brush replacement](../procedure/wi-gen-014.md) work order completed and a leading-edge erosion finding on blade C.[^c15][^c16]

## Facts

- On 2025-01-21 at -24.6 °C, [WTG-02](wtg-02.md) and WTG-09 needed more than 6 hours of heating before starting.[^c17]
- Finding F-04 is category 3 leading-edge erosion on WTG-09 blade C, with erosion between radius 50 m and 57 m, exposed laminate over 0.3 m, and a required leading-edge repair within 6 months.[^c15]
- WTG-09 has [gearbox](../component/gearbox.md) oil conversion to [PWG 220](../product/pwg-220.md) pending and deferred under [SB-LWS-2025-07](../bulletin/sb-lws-2025-07.md).[^c13]
- On 2026-04-01, icing alarms [A7001](../alarm-code/a7001.md) led to [S600 Stopped - icing](../status-code/s600.md) status starts for [WTG-12](wtg-12.md), WTG-09, [WTG-03](wtg-03.md), and WTG-02, with later clears and S600 ends recorded for each turbine in this part.[^c18][^c19][^c20][^c21][^c22][^c23][^c24][^c25]
- On 2026-04-02, [WTG-08](wtg-08.md) and WTG-09 raised [A2120 Gearbox oil pressure low](../alarm-code/a2120.md) at 0.59 bar and later cleared at 1.35 bar.[^c26][^c27][^c28][^c29]
- WTG-09 had [A3210 Generator brush wear limit reached](../alarm-code/a3210.md) raised on 2026-07-16 and cleared on 2026-07-18; WTG-09 was also in [S310 Maintenance mode](../status-code/s310.md) on 2026-07-18 from 07:58:12 to 11:52:40.[^c30][^c31][^c32][^c33]
- The movements sheet records a 2026-07-18 issue of quantity -1 for [LWS-P-51207](../part/lws-p-51207.md) against WO-26-0436 for WTG-09.[^c34]
- The WTG-09 gearbox oil conversion work order WO-25-0309 was deferred because the access road was closed by snow on 2025-11-24 and was completed on 2026-01-20.[^c14]
- WO-26-0436 for WTG-09 replaced [generator brushes](../component/generator-brushes.md) for A3210, was completed under [WI-GEN-014](../procedure/wi-gen-014.md), used part LWS-P-51207 quantity 1, and notes the shortest brush was 16 mm.[^c16]
- WTG-09 in 2026-01 recorded availability_pct 96.1, energy_mwh 955.9, downtime_h 29.0, and alarm_count 15.[^c35]
- The turbine labels shown on the plan are [WTG-01](wtg-01.md), WTG-02, WTG-03, [WTG-04](wtg-04.md), [WTG-05](wtg-05.md), [WTG-06](wtg-06.md), [WTG-07](wtg-07.md), WTG-08, WTG-09, [WTG-10](wtg-10.md), [WTG-11](wtg-11.md), and WTG-12.[^c1][^c2][^c3][^c4][^c5][^c6][^c7][^c8][^c9][^c10][^c11][^c12]
- WTG-09 had its generator brushes replaced under work order WO-26-0436 following alarm A3210 from 16/07.[^c36]
- For WTG-09, the shortest measured brush was B3 at 16 mm, below the 18 mm threshold in WI-GEN-014.[^c36]
- For WTG-09, a complete LWS-P-51207 set was replaced and the brush-holder screws were tightened to 12 N·m.[^c36]
- WTG-09 was put through run-in at 50 % power for 8 hours, until 20:00 that evening.[^c36]
- The next team was asked to check the end of the WTG-09 run-in at 20:00 and return it to 100 % power.[^c37]
- PWG 220 conversion status was: nine turbines completed, WTG-11 being converted that day, WTG-12 scheduled for the following Wednesday, and WTG-09 planned for the twenty-fourth if the access road remained open.[^c38][^c39]
- From minus twenty, heaters need about four hours to bring the sump to plus five; the previous January, WTG-02 and WTG-09 needed more than six hours.[^c40]
- If WTG-09 still has [PWG 320](../product/pwg-320.md) in January, its start temperature remains plus ten until conversion is complete; the parameter is changed only with the new oil.[^c41]
