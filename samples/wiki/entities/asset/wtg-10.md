# WTG-10

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

WTG-10 is a wind turbine listed on the [Quellmoor Ridge wind farm](quellmoor-ridge-wind-farm.md) site layout plan.[^c1][^c2][^c3][^c4][^c5][^c6][^c7][^c8][^c9][^c10][^c11][^c12] In 2026-H1, WTG-10 recorded 93.8% availability, 807.4 MWh of energy, 46.1 downtime hours, and 24 alarms.[^c13] WTG-10 had a high-priority corrective work order, WO-26-0187, that replaced the generator-side [HSS bearing](../component/hss-bearing.md) and used part [FGT-P-22328](../part/fgt-p-22328.md).[^c14][^c15]

## Facts

- WTG-10 raised [A4105 Pitch system fault](../alarm-code/a4105.md) with value 2 axis on 2026-08-23 01:43:03, entered [S510 Stopped - fault](../status-code/s510.md), cleared A4105 at 04:03:03, and ended S510 at 04:03:33.[^c16][^c17][^c18][^c19]
- The movements sheet records a 2026-03-12 issue of quantity -1 for [FGT-P-22328](../part/fgt-p-22328.md) against WO-26-0187 for WTG-10.[^c15]
- WO-26-0187 for WTG-10 replaced the generator-side [HSS bearing](../component/hss-bearing.md), was completed as high-priority corrective maintenance, used part FGT-P-22328 quantity 1, and notes that the last spare bearing was used.[^c14]
- WTG-10 in 2026-03 recorded availability_pct 93.8, energy_mwh 807.4, downtime_h 46.1, and alarm_count 24.[^c13]
- There had been no HSS bearing FGT-P-22328 in stock since the WTG-10 repair in March.[^c20]
- The turbine labels shown on the plan are [WTG-01](wtg-01.md), [WTG-02](wtg-02.md), [WTG-03](wtg-03.md), [WTG-04](wtg-04.md), [WTG-05](wtg-05.md), [WTG-06](wtg-06.md), [WTG-07](wtg-07.md), [WTG-08](wtg-08.md), [WTG-09](wtg-09.md), WTG-10, [WTG-11](wtg-11.md), and [WTG-12](wtg-12.md).[^c1][^c2][^c3][^c4][^c5][^c6][^c7][^c8][^c9][^c10][^c11][^c12]
- The last HSS bearing was used on WTG-10 in March under WO-26-0187, and [Fabrikam](../organisation/fabrikam-getriebetechnik.md) confirmed delivery of two bearings in week 39, from 21 to 25 September 2026.[^c21]
