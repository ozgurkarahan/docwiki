# WTG-02

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

WTG-02 is a turbine at [Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm](quellmoor-ridge-wind-farm.md).[^c1][^c2][^c3][^c4][^c5][^c6][^c7][^c8][^c9][^c10][^c11][^c12] Its 2026 maintenance plan includes [gearbox](../component/gearbox.md) endoscope inspection task MP-GBX-04, and the plan records that the task is done at 2026-07-01.[^c13][^c14] WTG-02 had a generator-side [HSS bearing](../component/hss-bearing.md) spalling finding on 2026-07-23, and work order WO-26-0439 is waiting for parts for the bearing replacement.[^c15][^c16]

## Facts

- On 2025-01-21 at -24.6 °C, WTG-02 and [WTG-09](wtg-09.md) needed more than 6 hours of heating before starting.[^c17]
- The audit method was document review, interviews, and observation of two jobs: a filter change on WTG-02 and a rescue drill on [WTG-06](wtg-06.md).[^c18]
- Issue 2 added task MP-GBX-07 for all turbines after INC-2026-006 and brought the WTG-02 endoscope inspection forward to July.[^c13]
- In the Plan 2026 sheet, WTG-02 [gearbox](../component/gearbox.md) endoscope inspection task MP-GBX-04 is planned for 2026-07 and has status done at 2026-07-01.[^c14]
- On 2026-04-01, icing alarms [A7001](../alarm-code/a7001.md) led to [S600 Stopped - icing](../status-code/s600.md) status starts for [WTG-12](wtg-12.md), WTG-09, [WTG-03](wtg-03.md), and WTG-02, with later clears and S600 ends recorded for each turbine in this part.[^c19][^c20][^c21][^c22][^c23][^c24][^c25][^c26]
- WTG-02 recorded [A2110 Gearbox HSS bearing temperature high](../alarm-code/a2110.md) twice in this part, with raised values of 91.2 °C on 2026-07-19 and 90.6 °C on 2026-07-22.[^c27][^c28]
- WTG-02 raised [A5001 Yaw misalignment](../alarm-code/a5001.md) at 14.7 deg and later cleared it at 4.2 deg on 2026-07-29.[^c29][^c30]
- WTG-02 and [WTG-05](wtg-05.md) entered [S310 Maintenance mode](../status-code/s310.md) on 2026-08-25 in the morning, and both maintenance mode intervals ended later the same day.[^c31][^c32][^c33][^c34]
- WO-26-0439 for WTG-02 is waiting for parts for a generator-side [HSS bearing](../component/hss-bearing.md) replacement after spalling was found at endoscopy on 2026-07-23, and it lists part [FGT-P-22328](../part/fgt-p-22328.md) quantity 1.[^c15]
- The turbine labels shown on the plan are [WTG-01](wtg-01.md), WTG-02, WTG-03, [WTG-04](wtg-04.md), WTG-05, WTG-06, [WTG-07](wtg-07.md), [WTG-08](wtg-08.md), WTG-09, [WTG-10](wtg-10.md), [WTG-11](wtg-11.md), and WTG-12.[^c1][^c2][^c3][^c4][^c5][^c6][^c7][^c8][^c9][^c10][^c11][^c12]
- WTG-02 had an endoscope inspection on 23/07 that found spalling on the HSS bearing on the [generator](../component/generator.md) side.[^c16]
- WO-26-0439 for WTG-02 was raised on 24/07 and is waiting for the same bearing as WTG-07, part FGT-P-22328.[^c16]
- WTG-02 is kept in operation and its vibration is reviewed weekly.[^c16]
- From minus twenty, heaters need about four hours to bring the sump to plus five; the previous January, WTG-02 and WTG-09 needed more than six hours.[^c35]
