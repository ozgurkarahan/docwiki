# WTG-11

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

WTG-11 is a turbine at [Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm](quellmoor-ridge-wind-farm.md).[^c1][^c2][^c3][^c4][^c5][^c6][^c7][^c8][^c9][^c10][^c11][^c12] Current records note a category 3 root gelcoat crack on blade C, a small hydraulic oil leak at the yaw brake hose, and a [gearbox oil sump temperature high](../alarm-code/a2101.md) alarm on WTG-11.[^c13][^c14][^c15][^c16] WTG-11 also had a dropped-object near miss at the yaw deck hatch, and corrective work installed tool tether anchor points there.[^c17][^c18]

## Facts

- Finding F-05 is a category 3 root gelcoat crack on WTG-11 blade C, located at radius 3.2 m, with repair within 6 months and a check at the next inspection.[^c13]
- Finding F5 was a minor finding: dropped-object exclusion signage was absent at the tower base of WTG-11 during nacelle work; the action was to provide a portable exclusion signage set for each turbine, owned by Callum Rydings and due 2026-03-31.[^c19]
- The document is a [Contoso Renewables](../organisation/contoso-renewables.md) incident management near-miss report numbered INC-2026-003 and titled "Dropped socket at WTG-11."[^c17]
- The incident was recorded as occurring on 2026-03-24 at 10:40 at WTG-11, yaw deck hatch, and was classified as a near miss involving a dropped object with high potential severity.[^c17]
- Corrective action A1 requires installing tool tether anchor points at the yaw deck hatches, first at WTG-11 under WO-26-0203 and then on all turbines; Callum Rydings owns the open action due 2026-04-30.[^c17]
- On 2026-04-01, WTG-11 raised [A5001 Yaw misalignment](../alarm-code/a5001.md) at 15.1 deg and cleared it at 4.2 deg.[^c20][^c21]
- WTG-11 raised [A2101 Gearbox oil sump temperature high](../alarm-code/a2101.md) at 75.1 °C on 2026-08-12 16:05:02 and cleared it at 72.7 °C on 2026-08-12 16:14:40.[^c14][^c15]
- During WTG-11 maintenance on 2026-08-16, [A1001 Emergency stop activated](../alarm-code/a1001.md) was raised at 10:05:47 and cleared at 10:16:47.[^c22][^c23]
- WO-26-0203 installed tool tether anchor points at the WTG-11 yaw deck hatch, was completed as high-priority corrective maintenance, and used part [AWI-31007](../part/awi-31007.md) quantity 1 under reference INC-2026-003.[^c18]
- Near the end of the export, WO-26-0444 for [WTG-05](wtg-05.md) is in progress for [rescue kit](../component/rescue-kit.md) inspection, WO-26-0450 for WTG-11 is waiting for parts for a leaking hydraulic hose in the yaw brake circuit with part [AWI-10442](../part/awi-10442.md) quantity 1, and WO-26-0457 for [WTG-03](wtg-03.md) is open for yaw brake pad inspection.[^c24][^c25][^c26]
- The turbine labels shown on the plan are [WTG-01](wtg-01.md), [WTG-02](wtg-02.md), WTG-03, [WTG-04](wtg-04.md), WTG-05, [WTG-06](wtg-06.md), [WTG-07](wtg-07.md), [WTG-08](wtg-08.md), [WTG-09](wtg-09.md), [WTG-10](wtg-10.md), WTG-11, and [WTG-12](wtg-12.md).[^c1][^c2][^c3][^c4][^c5][^c6][^c7][^c8][^c9][^c10][^c11][^c12]
- WTG-11 had a small hydraulic oil leak at a yaw brake hose found on the day of the handover.[^c16]
- The AWI-10442 hose for WTG-11 is not stocked and an order is to be raised.[^c16]
- The WTG-11 leak was contained with absorbent pads and is checked every shift.[^c16]
- [PWG 220](../product/pwg-220.md) conversion status was: nine turbines completed, WTG-11 being converted that day, WTG-12 scheduled for the following Wednesday, and WTG-09 planned for the twenty-fourth if the access road remained open.[^c27][^c28]
