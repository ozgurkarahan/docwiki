# Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm is a wind farm with 12 wind turbines, [WTG-01](wtg-01.md) to [WTG-12](wtg-12.md), and a total capacity of 38.4 MW.[^c1][^c2] It is operated by [Contoso Renewables](../organisation/contoso-renewables.md).[^c3][^c4][^c5][^c6][^c7] The site includes a 33/132 kV substation and a [Quellmoor Ridge O&M building](om-building.md).[^c8][^c9][^c10][^c11]

## Facts

- Environmental permit EP-2018-0417 applies to Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and is a consolidated version incorporating variation 1 and variation 2.[^c1]
- The installation is Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm, comprising 12 wind turbines [WTG-01](wtg-01.md) to [WTG-12](wtg-12.md) with a capacity of 38.4 MW.[^c1]
- The instruction is addressed to [Contoso Renewables](../organisation/contoso-renewables.md) for Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm.[^c3]
