# WTG-03

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

WTG-03 is a turbine listed on the [Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm](quellmoor-ridge-wind-farm.md) site layout plan.[^c1][^c2][^c3][^c4][^c5][^c6][^c7][^c8][^c9][^c10][^c11][^c12] It is part of the environmental permit curtailment group with [WTG-04](wtg-04.md) and [WTG-05](wtg-05.md).[^c13] Current records also show bat curtailment, yaw brake pad inspection, and a [yaw misalignment](../alarm-code/a5001.md) alarm for WTG-03.[^c14][^c15][^c16][^c17][^c18][^c19][^c20][^c21][^c22][^c23][^c24][^c25][^c26][^c27][^c28][^c29][^c30][^c31][^c32][^c33][^c34]

## Facts

- From 1 June to 31 August each year, WTG-03, [WTG-04](wtg-04.md) and [WTG-05](wtg-05.md) must be stopped between 22:00 and 05:00 local time whenever hub-height wind speed is below 6.0 m/s and air temperature is 10 °C or higher.[^c14]
- Variation 2 was justified by the presence of a protected bat species maternity roost in Skelvane Wood, east of WTG-03, WTG-04 and WTG-05, with foraging near the turbines on warm, calm summer nights.[^c14]
- On 2026-04-01, icing alarms [A7001](../alarm-code/a7001.md) led to [S600 Stopped - icing](../status-code/s600.md) status starts for [WTG-12](wtg-12.md), [WTG-09](wtg-09.md), WTG-03, and [WTG-02](wtg-02.md), with later clears and S600 ends recorded for each turbine in this part.[^c35][^c36][^c37][^c38][^c39][^c40][^c41][^c42]
- WTG-03, WTG-04, and WTG-05 started [S420 Curtailed - environmental condition](../status-code/s420.md) around 22:00 on 2026-06-01 and ended the curtailment around 05:00 on 2026-06-02.[^c15][^c16][^c17][^c18][^c19][^c20]
- WTG-03, WTG-04 and WTG-05 entered S420 Curtailed - environmental condition at 22:00 on 2026-08-25, and the curtailment ended at 05:00 on 2026-08-26.[^c21][^c22][^c23][^c24][^c25][^c26]
- WTG-03 had [A5001 Yaw misalignment](../alarm-code/a5001.md) raised at 18.9 deg and cleared at 4.2 deg on 2026-08-31.[^c27][^c28]
- Near the end of the export, WO-26-0444 for WTG-05 is in progress for [rescue kit](../component/rescue-kit.md) inspection, WO-26-0450 for [WTG-11](wtg-11.md) is waiting for parts for a leaking hydraulic hose in the yaw brake circuit with part [AWI-10442](../part/awi-10442.md) quantity 1, and WO-26-0457 for WTG-03 is open for yaw brake pad inspection.[^c29][^c30][^c31]
- The bat protection curtailment season started on 1 June for WTG-03, WTG-04, and WTG-05 under permit condition 7.3.[^c32]
- The turbine labels shown on the plan are [WTG-01](wtg-01.md), WTG-02, WTG-03, WTG-04, WTG-05, [WTG-06](wtg-06.md), [WTG-07](wtg-07.md), [WTG-08](wtg-08.md), WTG-09, [WTG-10](wtg-10.md), WTG-11, and WTG-12.[^c1][^c2][^c3][^c4][^c5][^c6][^c7][^c8][^c9][^c10][^c11][^c12]
- WTG-03 to WTG-05 are labelled as an environmental permit curtailment group.[^c13]
- WTG-03, WTG-04, and WTG-05 are expected to have bat curtailment again at night due to low wind.[^c33]
- WTG-03, WTG-04 and WTG-05 are expected to stop from 22:00 if wind remains low due to bat curtailment, and this should not be reset.[^c34]
- Radiator cleaning MP-GBX-07 on WTG-03 was postponed to next week.[^c43]
