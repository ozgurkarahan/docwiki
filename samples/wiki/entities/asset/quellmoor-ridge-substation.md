# Quellmoor Ridge substation

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

Quellmoor Ridge substation is a 33/132 kV substation with one 45 MVA transformer.[^c1] It is located next to the O&M building and connects to [City Power & Light](../organisation/city-power-and-light.md) and Skelvane 132 kV substation, circuit 2.[^c1] The substation low-voltage boards are covered by the [lockout/tagout procedure](../procedure/sop-loto-001.md) for work on the wind turbines and the substation.[^c2]

## Facts

- The procedure applies to all work on [WTG-01](wtg-01.md) to [WTG-12](wtg-12.md) and on the Quellmoor Ridge substation low-voltage boards.[^c2]
- The Quellmoor Ridge substation is a 33/132 kV substation with one 45 MVA transformer, located next to the O&M building, with connection to [City Power & Light](../organisation/city-power-and-light.md), Skelvane 132 kV substation, circuit 2.[^c1]
