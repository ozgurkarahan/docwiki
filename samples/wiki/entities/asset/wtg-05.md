# WTG-05

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

WTG-05 is a turbine at [Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm](quellmoor-ridge-wind-farm.md) and is part of the environmental permit curtailment group [WTG-03](wtg-03.md) to WTG-05.[^c1][^c2][^c3][^c4][^c5][^c6][^c7][^c8][^c9][^c10][^c11][^c12][^c13] It is subject to bat protection curtailment at night from 1 June to 31 August when wind is low and temperature is 10 °C or higher.[^c14][^c15] Current records also show a [rescue kit](../component/rescue-kit.md) inspection work order in progress for WTG-05.[^c16][^c17][^c18][^c19]

## Facts

- From 1 June to 31 August each year, [WTG-03](wtg-03.md), [WTG-04](wtg-04.md) and WTG-05 must be stopped between 22:00 and 05:00 local time whenever hub-height wind speed is below 6.0 m/s and air temperature is 10 °C or higher.[^c14]
- Variation 2 was justified by the presence of a protected bat species maternity roost in Skelvane Wood, east of WTG-03, WTG-04 and WTG-05, with foraging near the turbines on warm, calm summer nights.[^c14]
- WTG-03, WTG-04, and WTG-05 started [S420 Curtailed - environmental condition](../status-code/s420.md) around 22:00 on 2026-06-01 and ended the curtailment around 05:00 on 2026-06-02.[^c20][^c21][^c22][^c23][^c24][^c25]
- WTG-05 raised [A4105 Pitch system fault](../alarm-code/a4105.md) with value 1 axis on 2026-08-03, started [S510 Stopped - fault](../status-code/s510.md), then cleared A4105 and ended S510 about two hours later.[^c26][^c27][^c28][^c29]
- [WTG-02](wtg-02.md) and WTG-05 entered [S310 Maintenance mode](../status-code/s310.md) on 2026-08-25 in the morning, and both maintenance mode intervals ended later the same day.[^c30][^c31][^c32][^c33]
- WTG-03, WTG-04 and WTG-05 entered S420 Curtailed - environmental condition at 22:00 on 2026-08-25, and the curtailment ended at 05:00 on 2026-08-26.[^c34][^c35][^c36][^c37][^c38][^c39]
- Near the end of the export, WO-26-0444 for WTG-05 is in progress for [rescue kit](../component/rescue-kit.md) inspection, WO-26-0450 for [WTG-11](wtg-11.md) is waiting for parts for a leaking hydraulic hose in the yaw brake circuit with part [AWI-10442](../part/awi-10442.md) quantity 1, and WO-26-0457 for WTG-03 is open for yaw brake pad inspection.[^c16][^c17][^c18]
- The bat protection curtailment season started on 1 June for WTG-03, WTG-04, and WTG-05 under permit condition 7.3.[^c15]
- The turbine labels shown on the plan are [WTG-01](wtg-01.md), WTG-02, WTG-03, WTG-04, WTG-05, [WTG-06](wtg-06.md), [WTG-07](wtg-07.md), [WTG-08](wtg-08.md), [WTG-09](wtg-09.md), [WTG-10](wtg-10.md), WTG-11, and [WTG-12](wtg-12.md).[^c1][^c2][^c3][^c4][^c5][^c6][^c7][^c8][^c9][^c10][^c11][^c12]
- WTG-03 to WTG-05 are labelled as an environmental permit curtailment group.[^c13]
- The completed checklist is dated 14/07/2026, identifies the turbine as WTG-05 in the OCR text, and lists technician C. Rydings.[^c40][^c41][^c42]
- WTG-05 rescue kit inspection WO-26-0444 has started and is to finish next week.[^c19]
- WTG-03, WTG-04, and WTG-05 are expected to have bat curtailment again at night due to low wind.[^c19]
- WTG-03, WTG-04 and WTG-05 are expected to stop from 22:00 if wind remains low due to bat curtailment, and this should not be reset.[^c43]
- The WTG-05 daily checklist CHK-05 was completed yesterday with all items OK.[^c44]
