# WTG-01

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

WTG-01 is a wind turbine at [Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm](quellmoor-ridge-wind-farm.md), which has 12 turbines identified WTG-01 to [WTG-12](wtg-12.md).[^c1][^c2][^c3][^c4][^c5][^c6][^c7][^c8][^c9][^c10][^c11][^c12][^c13][^c14] The site’s turbines are [Litware LT-32](../product/lt-32.md) CCV units with [Fabrikam FGT-3200](../product/fgt-3200.md) gearboxes, and WTG-01 is included in site procedures for [gearbox](../component/gearbox.md) oil changes, lockout/tagout, and working at height.[^c15][^c16][^c17][^c18][^c2][^c19][^c20][^c21] For WTG-01, the 2026-H1 KPI export lists 98.6% availability, 952.7 MWh energy, 10.4 hours downtime, and 24 alarms for 2026-01.[^c22]

## Facts

- The installation is [Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm](quellmoor-ridge-wind-farm.md), comprising 12 wind turbines WTG-01 to [WTG-12](wtg-12.md) with a capacity of 38.4 MW.[^c1]
- The change was raised on 2025-05-06 by Dario Penwarden, O&M Supervisor, and is a technical lubricant change scoped to WTG-01 through WTG-12.[^c23][^c24][^c25]
- Action 3 is the conversion campaign for WTG-01 to WTG-12, owned by Dario Penwarden and due from 2025-09-01 to 2025-11-28.[^c26]
- The procedure applies to WTG-01 to WTG-12 with [Litware LT-32](../product/lt-32.md) CCV turbines and [Fabrikam FGT-3200](../product/fgt-3200.md) gearboxes.[^c15]
- The scope covers all [gearbox](../component/gearbox.md) oil changes on WTG-01 to WTG-12, including planned changes and changes decided after oil analysis.[^c16]
- The procedure applies to all work on WTG-01 to WTG-12 and on the [Quellmoor Ridge substation](quellmoor-ridge-substation.md) low-voltage boards.[^c17]
- The scope covers all climbing, work in the nacelle and hub, and rescue on WTG-01 to WTG-12.[^c18]
- [WI-GEN-014](../procedure/wi-gen-014.md) is a Revision A work instruction applicable from 2025-09-15 to WTG-01 through WTG-12 with a 690 V double-fed asynchronous [generator](../component/generator.md).[^c27][^c28]
- [MM-01 met mast](mm-01.md) is a 92 m meteorological mast located north of WTG-01 and is the reference for power curve tests.[^c29]
- On 2026-04-10, [S200 Ready - low wind](../status-code/s200.md) started for WTG-01 through WTG-12 between 11:00:11 and 11:01:28 and ended between 16:00:13 and 16:01:30.[^c30][^c31][^c32][^c33]
- On 2026-06-06, [A6101 Grid voltage out of range](../alarm-code/a6101.md) alarms were raised across WTG-01 through WTG-12 with value 87.2 %, accompanied by [S510 Stopped - fault](../status-code/s510.md) START entries; later A6101 clear entries show value 99.6 % and S510 END entries.[^c34][^c35][^c36][^c37][^c38][^c39]
- On 2026-06-18 at about 14:10, A6101 Grid voltage out of range alarms were raised on WTG-01 through WTG-12 at 89.2 %, with S510 Stopped - fault status starts recorded for each turbine in the same sequence.[^c40][^c41][^c42][^c43][^c44][^c45][^c46][^c47][^c48][^c49][^c50][^c51]
- On 2026-07-14, WTG-01 through WTG-12 started [S430 Curtailed - grid operator setpoint](../status-code/s430.md) at 20.0 MW; END entries are shown through WTG-12 across the part boundary.[^c52][^c53][^c54][^c55]
- On 2026-07-15 and 2026-07-16, S430 Curtailed - grid operator setpoint at 20.0 MW started for WTG-01 through WTG-12 at about 08:00 and ended at about 18:00.[^c56][^c57][^c58][^c59]
- On 2026-07-24, WTG-01 through WTG-12 entered S200 Ready - low wind status around 07:00 at 2.2 wind_speed_ms and ended S200 around 12:00 at 3.6 wind_speed_ms.[^c60][^c61][^c62][^c63]
- On 2026-08-02, A6101 Grid voltage out of range was raised for WTG-01 through WTG-12 with value 89.3 %, S510 Stopped - fault started, then the A6101 alarms cleared at 99.6 % and S510 ended.[^c64][^c65][^c66][^c67][^c68][^c69]
- On 2026-08-21, WTG-01 through WTG-12 raised A6101 Grid voltage out of range at 87.8 %, entered S510 Stopped - fault, then cleared A6101 at 99.6 % and ended S510.[^c70][^c71][^c72][^c73][^c74][^c75][^c76][^c77]
- On 2026-08-23, WTG-01 through WTG-12 started S200 Ready - low wind at wind speed 2.4 m/s and later ended S200 at wind speed 3.3 m/s.[^c78][^c79][^c80][^c81]
- The export records a completed high-priority modification campaign for gearbox oil conversion from [PWG 320](../product/pwg-320.md) to [PWG 220](../product/pwg-220.md) with a 60 L flush for WTG-01 through WTG-12 under reference MOC-2025-012; each row lists part [PWS-220-D208](../part/pws-220-d208.md) with quantity 3.[^c82][^c83][^c84][^c85][^c86][^c87][^c88][^c89][^c90][^c91][^c92][^c93]
- [Pitch bearing](../component/pitch-bearing.md) bolt re-torque work for WTG-01 through [WTG-06](wtg-06.md) was completed with a stated torque of 1,450 Nm and coverage of 100 % of bolts, referenced to [SB-LWS-2025-11](../bulletin/sb-lws-2025-11.md) Rev. A.[^c94][^c95][^c96][^c97][^c98][^c99]
- Gearbox oil sampling work orders WO-26-0341 through WO-26-0352 cover WTG-01 through WTG-12 and are all marked Completed.[^c100][^c101][^c102][^c103][^c104][^c105][^c106][^c107][^c108][^c109][^c110][^c111]
- The first listed record is for WTG-01 in 2026-01, with availability_pct 98.6, energy_mwh 952.7, downtime_h 10.4, and alarm_count 24.[^c22]
- Quellmoor Ridge has 12 Litware LT-32 turbines in CCV configuration, identified WTG-01 to WTG-12, with total capacity of 38.4 MW.[^c2]
- The turbine labels shown on the plan are WTG-01, [WTG-02](wtg-02.md), [WTG-03](wtg-03.md), [WTG-04](wtg-04.md), [WTG-05](wtg-05.md), WTG-06, [WTG-07](wtg-07.md), [WTG-08](wtg-08.md), [WTG-09](wtg-09.md), [WTG-10](wtg-10.md), [WTG-11](wtg-11.md), and WTG-12.[^c3][^c4][^c5][^c6][^c7][^c8][^c9][^c10][^c11][^c12][^c13][^c14]
- The configuration records checked for Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm showed that all 12 turbines, WTG-01 through WTG-12, were delivered as LT-32 in CCV configuration with Fabrikam FGT-3200 gearboxes, so [SB-LWS-2025-07](../bulletin/sb-lws-2025-07.md) applies to all 12 turbines.[^c19]
- [Rescue kit](../component/rescue-kit.md) inspections were performed on WTG-01 to WTG-04 under MP-HSE-01.[^c112]
- Superseded: The procedure applies to WTG-01 to WTG-12, specified as Litware LT-32 CCV turbines with Fabrikam FGT-3200 gearboxes.[^c20]
- Superseded: The scope covers all gearbox oil changes on WTG-01 to WTG-12, whether planned or decided after oil analysis.[^c21]
