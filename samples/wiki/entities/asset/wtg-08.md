# WTG-08

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

WTG-08 is a wind turbine at [Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm](quellmoor-ridge-wind-farm.md).[^c1][^c2][^c3][^c4][^c5][^c6][^c7][^c8][^c9][^c10][^c11][^c12] A current blade inspection found category 3 lightning receptor damage on WTG-08 blade A, with the tip receptor burnt.[^c13] The site SCADA log recorded an [A2120 Gearbox oil pressure low](../alarm-code/a2120.md) alarm for WTG-08 on 2026-04-02, which later cleared.[^c14][^c15][^c16][^c17]

## Facts

- Finding F-03 is category 3 lightning receptor damage on WTG-08 blade A; the tip receptor is burnt, the receptor is to be replaced within 6 months, and continuity of the down conductor is to be checked.[^c13]
- On 2026-04-02, WTG-08 and [WTG-09](wtg-09.md) raised [A2120 Gearbox oil pressure low](../alarm-code/a2120.md) at 0.59 bar and later cleared at 1.35 bar.[^c14][^c15][^c16][^c17]
- The turbine labels shown on the plan are [WTG-01](wtg-01.md), [WTG-02](wtg-02.md), [WTG-03](wtg-03.md), [WTG-04](wtg-04.md), [WTG-05](wtg-05.md), [WTG-06](wtg-06.md), [WTG-07](wtg-07.md), WTG-08, WTG-09, [WTG-10](wtg-10.md), [WTG-11](wtg-11.md), and [WTG-12](wtg-12.md).[^c1][^c2][^c3][^c4][^c5][^c6][^c7][^c8][^c9][^c10][^c11][^c12]
