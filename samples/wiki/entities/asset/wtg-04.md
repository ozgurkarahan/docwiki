# WTG-04

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

WTG-04 is a turbine at [Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm](quellmoor-ridge-wind-farm.md).[^c1][^c2][^c3][^c4][^c5][^c6][^c7][^c8][^c9][^c10][^c11][^c12] It is subject to bat-protection curtailment and monitoring requirements under the current environmental permit.[^c13][^c14] In 2026, WTG-04 had a category 4 trailing-edge crack on blade B and was scheduled for replacement.[^c15][^c16][^c17]

## Facts

- Bat activity must be monitored at the nacelle of WTG-04 with an acoustic detector from April to October each year.[^c13]
- From 1 June to 31 August each year, [WTG-03](wtg-03.md), WTG-04 and [WTG-05](wtg-05.md) must be stopped between 22:00 and 05:00 local time whenever hub-height wind speed is below 6.0 m/s and air temperature is 10 °C or higher.[^c13]
- Variation 2 was justified by the presence of a protected bat species maternity roost in Skelvane Wood, east of WTG-03, WTG-04 and WTG-05, with foraging near the turbines on warm, calm summer nights.[^c13]
- WTG-04 had the highest overall blade category in the turbine summary: blade A category 2, blade B category 4, blade C category 2, and highest category 4.[^c18]
- Finding F-01 is a category 4 trailing-edge crack on WTG-04 blade B.[^c15]
- For F-01, repair is not recommended at that location and WTG-04 blade B is to be replaced before 2026-09-30.[^c15]
- Until WTG-04 blade B is replaced, the crack is to be checked by drone every month and the turbine is to be stopped immediately if the crack exceeds 1.2 m.[^c15]
- Finding F-02 is category 3 leading-edge erosion on WTG-04 blade B, with coating loss between radius 52 m and 57 m and no exposed laminate.[^c15]
- The recommendations are to replace WTG-04 blade B before 2026-09-30 using a crane lift, repair category 3 findings within 6 months using rope access, and perform the next full inspection in May 2027.[^c15]
- The job is the WTG-04 [rotor blade](../component/rotor-blade.md) B replacement, described as a single blade lift.[^c19]
- The maximum gust is 11 m/s; no lift is allowed in fog with visibility below 300 m, lightning within 15 km, or icing, and wind is read at the WTG-04 nacelle anemometer and boom tip anemometer.[^c19]
- The exclusion zone radius is 150 m around WTG-04 during the lift.[^c19]
- WTG-03, WTG-04, and WTG-05 started [S420 Curtailed - environmental condition](../status-code/s420.md) around 22:00 on 2026-06-01 and ended the curtailment around 05:00 on 2026-06-02.[^c20][^c21][^c22][^c23][^c24][^c25]
- WTG-04 entered [S310 Maintenance mode](../status-code/s310.md) on 2026-08-07, raised and cleared [A1001 Emergency stop activated](../alarm-code/a1001.md) during that maintenance period, and then ended S310.[^c26][^c27][^c28][^c29]
- WTG-04 raised [A2140 Gearbox oil filter differential pressure high](../alarm-code/a2140.md) at 2.89 bar on 2026-08-11 17:18:08 and cleared it at 1.90 bar on 2026-08-11 18:22:08.[^c30][^c31]
- WTG-03, WTG-04 and WTG-05 entered S420 Curtailed - environmental condition at 22:00 on 2026-08-25, and the curtailment ended at 05:00 on 2026-08-26.[^c32][^c33][^c34][^c35][^c36][^c37]
- WO-26-0355 for WTG-04 is a planned high-priority corrective-maintenance work order to replace rotor blade B for a trailing-edge crack, with planned date 2026-09-08 and references WBS-2026-031 and LP-2026-009.[^c16]
- WTG-04 blade B had a category 4 trailing-edge crack and had to be replaced before 2026-09-30 under WO-26-0355.[^c17]
- The crane lift for the WTG-04 blade replacement was to be planned with [Tailspin Heavy Lift](../organisation/tailspin-heavy-lift.md) for early September.[^c17]
- The bat protection curtailment season started on 1 June for WTG-03, WTG-04, and WTG-05 under permit condition 7.3.[^c14]
- The turbine labels shown on the plan are [WTG-01](wtg-01.md), [WTG-02](wtg-02.md), WTG-03, WTG-04, WTG-05, [WTG-06](wtg-06.md), [WTG-07](wtg-07.md), [WTG-08](wtg-08.md), [WTG-09](wtg-09.md), [WTG-10](wtg-10.md), [WTG-11](wtg-11.md), and [WTG-12](wtg-12.md).[^c1][^c2][^c3][^c4][^c5][^c6][^c7][^c8][^c9][^c10][^c11][^c12]
- WTG-04 received a ground-based visual inspection of blade B, with nothing new noted.[^c38]
- WTG-03, WTG-04, and WTG-05 are expected to have bat curtailment again at night due to low wind.[^c39]
- WTG-03, WTG-04 and WTG-05 are expected to stop from 22:00 if wind remains low due to bat curtailment, and this should not be reset.[^c40]
- On WTG-04, the 2026-07-02 drone check of blade B found the crack unchanged at 0.9 m.[^c40]
- [Rescue kit](../component/rescue-kit.md) inspections were performed on WTG-01 to WTG-04 under MP-HSE-01.[^c41]
