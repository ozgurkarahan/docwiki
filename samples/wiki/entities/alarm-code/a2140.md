# A2140 Gearbox oil filter differential pressure high

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

A2140 is the gearbox oil filter differential pressure high alarm.[^c1] It is raised at 2.5 bar and can be caused by a clogged filter element or cold oil.[^c1] The stated action is to replace filter element [FGT-P-11872](../part/fgt-p-11872.md).[^c1]

## Facts

- The filter element [FGT-P-11872](../part/fgt-p-11872.md) must be replaced every 12 months or at alarm A2140, and breather [FGT-P-11880](../part/fgt-p-11880.md) must be replaced every 12 months.[^c2]
- Alarm A2140, Gearbox oil filter differential pressure high at 2.5 bar, can be caused by a clogged filter element or cold oil; the action is to replace filter element FGT-P-11872.[^c1]
- [WTG-12](../asset/wtg-12.md) raised A2140 Gearbox oil filter differential pressure high at 2.56 bar on 2026-08-02 and cleared it at 1.90 bar later the same day.[^c3][^c4]
- [WTG-04](../asset/wtg-04.md) raised A2140 Gearbox oil filter differential pressure high at 2.89 bar on 2026-08-11 17:18:08 and cleared it at 1.90 bar on 2026-08-11 18:22:08.[^c5][^c6]
- [WTG-06](../asset/wtg-06.md) had A2140 Gearbox oil filter differential pressure high raised at 2.89 bar and cleared at 1.90 bar on 2026-08-31.[^c7][^c8]
- For A2140 filter alarm in cold conditions, thick oil can cause a high pressure drop across the filter; wait for the oil to warm up and then check again.[^c9][^c10]
