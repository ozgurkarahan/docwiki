# A6101 Grid voltage out of range

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

A6101 Grid voltage out of range is an alarm code recorded for [WTG-01](../asset/wtg-01.md) through [WTG-12](../asset/wtg-12.md) in the SCADA alarm and status log.[^c1][^c2][^c3][^c4][^c5][^c6][^c7][^c8][^c9][^c10][^c11][^c12][^c13][^c14][^c15][^c16][^c17][^c18][^c19][^c20][^c21][^c22][^c23][^c24][^c25][^c26][^c27][^c28][^c29][^c30][^c31][^c32] The log shows A6101 alarms and corresponding [S510 Stopped - fault](../status-code/s510.md) status entries, with clear entries also recorded.[^c33][^c34][^c35][^c36][^c1][^c2][^c3][^c4][^c5][^c6][^c7][^c8][^c9][^c10][^c11][^c12][^c13][^c14][^c15][^c16][^c17][^c18][^c19][^c20][^c21][^c22][^c23][^c24][^c25][^c26][^c27][^c28][^c29][^c30][^c31][^c32]

## Facts

- On 2026-04-20, A6101 Grid voltage out of range cleared at 99.6 %, with corresponding [S510 Stopped - fault](../status-code/s510.md) END entries recorded between 20:49:12 and 20:55:06.[^c33][^c34][^c35][^c36]
- On 2026-06-06, A6101 Grid voltage out of range alarms were raised across [WTG-01](../asset/wtg-01.md) through [WTG-12](../asset/wtg-12.md) with value 87.2 %, accompanied by S510 Stopped - fault START entries; later A6101 clear entries show value 99.6 % and S510 END entries.[^c1][^c2][^c3][^c4][^c5][^c6]
- On 2026-06-18 at about 14:10, A6101 Grid voltage out of range alarms were raised on WTG-01 through WTG-12 at 89.2 %, with S510 Stopped - fault status starts recorded for each turbine in the same sequence.[^c7][^c8][^c9][^c10][^c11][^c12][^c13][^c14][^c15][^c16][^c17][^c18]
- On 2026-08-02, A6101 Grid voltage out of range was raised for WTG-01 through WTG-12 with value 89.3 %, S510 Stopped - fault started, then the A6101 alarms cleared at 99.6 % and S510 ended.[^c19][^c20][^c21][^c22][^c23][^c24]
- On 2026-08-21, WTG-01 through WTG-12 raised A6101 Grid voltage out of range at 87.8 %, entered S510 Stopped - fault, then cleared A6101 at 99.6 % and ended S510.[^c25][^c26][^c27][^c28][^c29][^c30][^c31][^c32]
