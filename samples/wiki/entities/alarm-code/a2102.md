# A2102 Gearbox oil sump temperature trip

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

A2102 Gearbox oil sump temperature trip is an alarm code for a gearbox oil sump temperature trip.[^c1][^c2][^c3] It trips at 82 °C and stops the turbine automatically.[^c4][^c1][^c2][^c3] The cooling circuit is inspected before restart, and the reset is not performed more than once without inspection.[^c2]

## Facts

- Warning [A2101](a2101.md) and trip A2102 temperature thresholds remain 75 °C and 82 °C.[^c4]
- Oil sump temperature warning A2101 is set at 75°C, and oil sump temperature trip A2102 is set at 82 °C.[^c1]
- Alarm A2102, Gearbox oil sump temperature trip at 82 °C, stops the turbine automatically; the cooling circuit is inspected before restart, and reset is not performed more than once without inspection.[^c2]
- The report was opened on 2026-05-30 after the first A2102 trip.[^c5]
- Between 2026-04-09 and 2026-06-21, [WTG-07](../asset/wtg-07.md) logged 27 A2101 warnings at 75 °C and two A2102 trips at 82 °C, while no other turbine logged an A2101 warning in that period.[^c6]
- On 2026-05-30, WTG-07 raised A2101 Gearbox oil sump temperature high at 75.3 °C, then raised A2102 Gearbox oil sump temperature trip at 82.6 °C and entered [S510 Stopped - fault](../status-code/s510.md).[^c7][^c8][^c9]
- WTG-07 cleared the 2026-05-30 A2102 trip at 79.4 °C, cleared the A2101 high-temperature alarm at 72.8 °C, and ended S510 Stopped - fault.[^c10][^c11][^c12]
- On 2026-06-19, WTG-07 first raised A2101 Gearbox oil sump temperature high at 75.6 °C, then raised A2102 Gearbox oil sump temperature trip at 83.4 °C and entered S510 Stopped - fault.[^c13][^c14][^c15]
- A2101 gives a warning at 75 °C, and A2102 trips at 82 °C.[^c3]
- The quiz answers state that the HSS drives the [generator](../component/generator.md), A2102 trips at 82 °C, the oil used is [Proseware PWG 220](../product/pwg-220.md), and a rising PQ index means more ferrous wear debris, often from a bearing.[^c16]
