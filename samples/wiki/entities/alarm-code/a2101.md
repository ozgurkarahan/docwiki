# A2101 Gearbox oil sump temperature high

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

A2101 is the gearbox oil sump temperature high alarm, with a warning threshold of 75 °C.[^c1][^c2] Its trip counterpart is [A2102](a2102.md) at 82 °C.[^c3][^c1][^c2] In SCADA logs, [WTG-07](../asset/wtg-07.md) and [WTG-11](../asset/wtg-11.md) raised A2101 at around 75 °C.[^c4][^c5][^c6][^c7][^c8][^c9][^c10][^c11][^c12]

## Facts

- Warning A2101 and trip [A2102](a2102.md) temperature thresholds remain 75 °C and 82 °C.[^c3]
- Oil sump temperature warning A2101 is set at 75°C, and oil sump temperature trip A2102 is set at 82 °C.[^c1]
- Alarm A2101, Gearbox oil sump temperature high at 75 °C, can be caused by cooling, oil level, load/ambient-temperature, or bearing issues; actions include checking fan rotation and motor current, cleaning the radiator, checking valve insert [LWS-P-30422](../part/lws-p-30422.md), checking oil level, reviewing vibration trend, and taking an oil sample.[^c13]
- Between 2026-04-09 and 2026-06-21, [WTG-07](../asset/wtg-07.md) logged 27 A2101 warnings at 75 °C and two A2102 trips at 82 °C, while no other turbine logged an A2101 warning in that period.[^c4]
- Verification reports no A2101 warning on WTG-07 since 2026-06-24, but vibration remains above the alarm level until the bearing is replaced.[^c14]
- On 2026-05-30, WTG-07 raised A2101 Gearbox oil sump temperature high at 75.3 °C, then raised A2102 Gearbox oil sump temperature trip at 82.6 °C and entered [S510 Stopped - fault](../status-code/s510.md).[^c5][^c6][^c7]
- WTG-07 cleared the 2026-05-30 A2102 trip at 79.4 °C, cleared the A2101 high-temperature alarm at 72.8 °C, and ended S510 Stopped - fault.[^c15][^c16][^c17]
- On 2026-06-19, WTG-07 first raised A2101 Gearbox oil sump temperature high at 75.6 °C, then raised A2102 Gearbox oil sump temperature trip at 83.4 °C and entered S510 Stopped - fault.[^c8][^c9][^c10]
- [WTG-11](../asset/wtg-11.md) raised A2101 Gearbox oil sump temperature high at 75.1 °C on 2026-08-12 16:05:02 and cleared it at 72.7 °C on 2026-08-12 16:14:40.[^c11][^c12]
- A2101 gives a warning at 75 °C, and A2102 trips at 82 °C.[^c2]
