# A7001 Ice detected on rotor blades

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

A7001 is an ice detection alarm for ice detected on rotor blades.[^c1][^c2] It stops the turbine automatically when active.[^c1][^c2] When ice is detected under A7001, there is no access within 300 m of a turbine.[^c3]

## Facts

- For ice, there is no access within 300 m of a turbine with ice detected under alarm A7001.[^c3]
- On 2026-04-01, icing alarms A7001 led to [S600 Stopped - icing](../status-code/s600.md) status starts for [WTG-12](../asset/wtg-12.md), [WTG-09](../asset/wtg-09.md), [WTG-03](../asset/wtg-03.md), and [WTG-02](../asset/wtg-02.md), with later clears and S600 ends recorded for each turbine in this part.[^c4][^c5][^c6][^c7][^c8][^c9][^c10][^c11]
- Ice detection alarm A7001 stops the turbine automatically.[^c1]
- When A7001 ice detection is active, the turbine stops by itself.[^c2]

## Timeline
