# A2110 Gearbox HSS bearing temperature high

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

A2110 is an alarm code for Gearbox HSS bearing temperature high.[^c1] It is raised at 90 °C.[^c1] In the SCADA log, [WTG-02](../asset/wtg-02.md) recorded A2110 twice, with values of 91.2 °C on 2026-07-19 and 90.6 °C on 2026-07-22.[^c2][^c3]

## Facts

- Alarm A2110, Gearbox HSS bearing temperature high at 90 °C, can be caused by bearing damage or lubrication starvation; checks are vibration trend review and endoscope inspection of the bearing.[^c1]
- [WTG-02](../asset/wtg-02.md) recorded A2110 Gearbox HSS bearing temperature high twice in this part, with raised values of 91.2 °C on 2026-07-19 and 90.6 °C on 2026-07-22.[^c2][^c3]
