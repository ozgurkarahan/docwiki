# A3210 Generator brush wear limit reached

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

A3210 is the alarm code for Generator brush wear limit reached.[^c1] It appears when the remaining brush length reaches approximately 20 mm.[^c1] Records for [WTG-09](../asset/wtg-09.md) show the alarm was raised on 2026-07-16 and cleared on 2026-07-18, and the [generator brushes](../component/generator-brushes.md) were replaced under work order WO-26-0436.[^c2][^c3][^c4][^c5][^c6][^c7]

## Facts

- Alarm A3210, Generator brush wear limit reached, appears when the remaining brush length reaches approximately 20 mm.[^c1]
- [WTG-09](../asset/wtg-09.md) had A3210 Generator brush wear limit reached raised on 2026-07-16 and cleared on 2026-07-18; WTG-09 was also in [S310 Maintenance mode](../status-code/s310.md) on 2026-07-18 from 07:58:12 to 11:52:40.[^c2][^c3][^c4][^c5]
- WO-26-0436 for WTG-09 replaced [generator brushes](../component/generator-brushes.md) for A3210, was completed under [WI-GEN-014](../procedure/wi-gen-014.md), used part [LWS-P-51207](../part/lws-p-51207.md) quantity 1, and notes the shortest brush was 16 mm.[^c6]
- WTG-09 had its generator brushes replaced under work order WO-26-0436 following alarm A3210 from 16/07.[^c7]

## Timeline

- 2026-07-16: [WTG-09](../asset/wtg-09.md) had A3210 Generator brush wear limit reached raised on 2026-07-16 and cleared on 2026-07-18; WTG-09 was also in [S310 Maintenance mode](../status-code/s310.md) on 2026-07-18 from 07:58:12 to 11:52:40.[^c2][^c3][^c4][^c5]
