# A2120 Gearbox oil pressure low

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

A2120 is an alarm code for gearbox oil pressure low.[^c1] It is associated with a 0.8 bar alarm threshold.[^c1] The alarm can occur after a cold start, and the listed causes include cold oil, pump fault, or a blocked filter.[^c1][^c2]

## Facts

- Alarm A2120, Gearbox oil pressure low at 0.8 bar, can be caused by cold oil at start-up, pump fault, or blocked filter; actions are to allow heater time at cold start and check the pump and filter.[^c1]
- On 2026-04-02, [WTG-08](../asset/wtg-08.md) and [WTG-09](../asset/wtg-09.md) raised A2120 Gearbox oil pressure low at 0.59 bar and later cleared at 1.35 bar.[^c3][^c4][^c5][^c6]
- For A2120 gearbox oil pressure low immediately after a cold start, technicians should give the heaters more time before escalating, because it is usually cold oil rather than a pump fault.[^c2]
