# A5001 Yaw misalignment

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

A5001 Yaw misalignment is an alarm code.[^c1][^c2][^c3][^c4][^c5][^c6][^c7][^c8][^c9][^c10] It was raised and later cleared on [WTG-11](../asset/wtg-11.md), [WTG-02](../asset/wtg-02.md), [WTG-06](../asset/wtg-06.md), and [WTG-03](../asset/wtg-03.md) in the SCADA alarm and status log.[^c1][^c2][^c3][^c4][^c5][^c6][^c7][^c8][^c9][^c10]

## Facts

- On 2026-04-01, [WTG-11](../asset/wtg-11.md) raised A5001 Yaw misalignment at 15.1 deg and cleared it at 4.2 deg.[^c1][^c2]
- [WTG-02](../asset/wtg-02.md) raised A5001 Yaw misalignment at 14.7 deg and later cleared it at 4.2 deg on 2026-07-29.[^c3][^c4]
- [WTG-06](../asset/wtg-06.md) had two A5001 Yaw misalignment raised records on 2026-07-30, at 11.8 deg and 16.5 deg, followed by two cleared records at 4.2 deg.[^c5][^c6][^c7][^c8]
- [WTG-03](../asset/wtg-03.md) had A5001 Yaw misalignment raised at 18.9 deg and cleared at 4.2 deg on 2026-08-31.[^c9][^c10]
