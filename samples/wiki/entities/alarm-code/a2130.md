# A2130 Gearbox oil level low

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

A2130 is an alarm code for gearbox oil level low.[^c1] If the [gearbox](../component/gearbox.md) oil level is below MIN, the turbine should not be restarted and the issue should be reported to the O&M Supervisor.[^c1]

## Facts

- If the level is below MIN, do not restart the turbine and report it to the O&M Supervisor because of the risk of alarm A2130.[^c1]
