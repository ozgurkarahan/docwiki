# A1001 Emergency stop activated

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

A1001 Emergency stop activated is an alarm code in the reviewed registry.[^c1][^c2][^c3][^c4][^c5][^c6] It was raised and cleared during maintenance on [WTG-04](../asset/wtg-04.md) and [WTG-11](../asset/wtg-11.md).[^c1][^c2][^c3][^c4][^c5][^c6]

## Facts

- [WTG-04](../asset/wtg-04.md) entered [S310 Maintenance mode](../status-code/s310.md) on 2026-08-07, raised and cleared A1001 Emergency stop activated during that maintenance period, and then ended S310.[^c1][^c2][^c3][^c4]
- During [WTG-11](../asset/wtg-11.md) maintenance on 2026-08-16, A1001 Emergency stop activated was raised at 10:05:47 and cleared at 10:16:47.[^c5][^c6]
