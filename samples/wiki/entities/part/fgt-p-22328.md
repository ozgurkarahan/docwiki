# FGT-P-22328

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

FGT-P-22328 is a spare part: the generator-side [HSS bearing](../component/hss-bearing.md) for [FGT-3200](../product/fgt-3200.md).[^c1][^c2] It is a critical item below minimum stock, with 0 on hand and 2 on order under PO-76994.[^c1][^c2][^c3] The bearing is needed for work orders WO-26-0412 on [WTG-07](../asset/wtg-07.md) and WO-26-0439 on [WTG-02](../asset/wtg-02.md), and WO-26-0187 on [WTG-10](../asset/wtg-10.md) already used one unit.[^c4][^c5][^c6][^c7]

## Facts

- Replacing the generator-side [HSS bearing](../component/hss-bearing.md) FGT-P-22328 under WO-26-0412 is waiting for parts and is planned on delivery of the bearing.[^c8]
- FGT-P-22328, the HSS bearing on the [generator](../component/generator.md) side for [FGT-3200](../product/fgt-3200.md), is a critical item below minimum stock: 0 pc on hand, minimum 1 pc, reorder quantity 2 pc, and 2 pc on order under PO-76994 for expected delivery on 2026-09-25.[^c1][^c2]
- The movements sheet records a 2026-03-12 issue of quantity -1 for FGT-P-22328 against WO-26-0187 for [WTG-10](../asset/wtg-10.md).[^c9]
- WO-26-0187 for WTG-10 replaced the generator-side HSS bearing, was completed as high-priority corrective maintenance, used part FGT-P-22328 quantity 1, and notes that the last spare bearing was used.[^c4]
- WO-26-0412 for [WTG-07](../asset/wtg-07.md) is a high-priority corrective-maintenance work order to replace the generator-side HSS bearing, is waiting for parts, lists part FGT-P-22328 quantity 1, and remarks that vibration is above the alarm level of 4.5 mm/s.[^c5]
- WO-26-0439 for [WTG-02](../asset/wtg-02.md) is waiting for parts for a generator-side HSS bearing replacement after spalling was found at endoscopy on 2026-07-23, and it lists part FGT-P-22328 quantity 1.[^c6]
- There had been no HSS bearing FGT-P-22328 in stock since the WTG-10 repair in March.[^c10]
- The action list assigned Tomasz Lebrecht to raise the purchase order for two HSS bearings FGT-P-22328 by 2026-06-22.[^c11]
- The supplier note is dated 03.08.2026 and is addressed to [Contoso Renewables](../organisation/contoso-renewables.md) at [Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm](../asset/quellmoor-ridge-wind-farm.md), with the subject PO-76994 for HSS bearings FGT-P-22328 and notes after the oil changeover.[^c12]
- The two HSS bearings FGT-P-22328 from PO-76994 cannot be delivered in calendar week 39.[^c13]
- The email was sent by Tomasz Lebrecht to Dario Penwarden on 22 July 2026 with the subject “Parts shortage - HSS bearing FGT-P-22328 below minimum stock.”[^c14]
- A stock snapshot dated 21 July showed that HSS bearing FGT-P-22328 on the generator side was below its minimum level.[^c15]
- For HSS bearing FGT-P-22328, stock was 0 on hand, minimum 1, and 2 on order under PO-76994 to [Fabrikam](../organisation/fabrikam-getriebetechnik.md).[^c3]
- WO-26-0439 for WTG-02 was raised on 24/07 and is waiting for the same bearing as WTG-07, part FGT-P-22328.[^c7]
