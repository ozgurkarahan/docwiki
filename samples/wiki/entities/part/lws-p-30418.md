# LWS-P-30418

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

LWS-P-30418 is a 1.5 kW [oil cooler fan motor](../component/cooler-fan-motor.md) spare part.[^c1][^c2] It was replaced on [WTG-07](../asset/wtg-07.md) under WO-26-0398, and the related [oil cooler](../component/oil-cooler.md) radiator was cleaned under WO-26-0399.[^c3][^c4][^c5][^c6] Its minimum stock was raised from 1 to 2, and it is currently below minimum with 1 on hand and 2 on order.[^c7][^c1][^c2][^c8]

## Facts

- The fan motor LWS-P-30418 bearing had failed and the motor current was 35 % above nominal.[^c9]
- Corrective actions completed on 2026-06-24 included replacing [oil cooler fan motor](../component/cooler-fan-motor.md) LWS-P-30418 under WO-26-0398 and cleaning the [oil cooler](../component/oil-cooler.md) radiator under WO-26-0399.[^c3][^c4]
- Minimum stock of fan motor LWS-P-30418 was raised from 1 to 2.[^c7]
- LWS-P-30418, the 1.5 kW oil cooler fan motor, is a critical item below minimum stock: 1 pc on hand, minimum 2 pc, reorder quantity 2 pc, and 2 pc on order under PO-77102 for expected delivery on 2026-08-14.[^c1][^c2]
- The movements sheet records a 2026-06-24 issue of quantity -1 for LWS-P-30418 against WO-26-0398 for [WTG-07](../asset/wtg-07.md).[^c10]
- For WTG-07 under INC-2026-006, WO-26-0398 replaced the oil cooler fan motor with part LWS-P-30418 quantity 1, and WO-26-0399 cleaned the oil cooler radiator with remarks that fins were about 40 % blocked by insects and pollen.[^c5][^c6]
- Oil cooler fan motor LWS-P-30418 is also below minimum after the WTG-07 repair, with 1 on hand and a minimum of 2.[^c8]
