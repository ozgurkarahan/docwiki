# LWS-P-55031

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

LWS-P-55031 is a slip-ring cleaning kit used for [generator brush replacement](../procedure/wi-gen-014.md).[^c1][^c2][^c3] It is used to clean the [slip ring](../component/slip-ring.md) before new brushes are installed and checked for free sliding.[^c2][^c3]

## Facts

- The slip-ring cleaning kit required is LWS-P-55031.[^c1]
- The [slip ring](../component/slip-ring.md) must be cleaned with kit LWS-P-55031, then new brushes must be installed and checked for free sliding.[^c2][^c3]
