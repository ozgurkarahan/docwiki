# LWS-P-51207

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

LWS-P-51207 is a spare part brush set that contains 18 brushes.[^c1] It was used for [generator brush replacement](../procedure/wi-gen-014.md) work on [WTG-09](../asset/wtg-09.md) under WO-26-0436 and WI-GEN-014.[^c2][^c3] A complete LWS-P-51207 set was replaced on WTG-09.[^c4]

## Facts

- The required brush set is LWS-P-51207 and contains 18 brushes.[^c1]
- The movements sheet records a 2026-07-18 issue of quantity -1 for LWS-P-51207 against WO-26-0436 for [WTG-09](../asset/wtg-09.md).[^c2]
- WO-26-0436 for WTG-09 replaced [generator brushes](../component/generator-brushes.md) for [A3210](../alarm-code/a3210.md), was completed under [WI-GEN-014](../procedure/wi-gen-014.md), used part LWS-P-51207 quantity 1, and notes the shortest brush was 16 mm.[^c3]
- For WTG-09, a complete LWS-P-51207 set was replaced and the brush-holder screws were tightened to 12 N·m.[^c4]

## Timeline

- 2026-07-18: The movements sheet records a 2026-07-18 issue of quantity -1 for LWS-P-51207 against WO-26-0436 for [WTG-09](../asset/wtg-09.md).[^c2]
