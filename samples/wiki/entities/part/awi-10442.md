# AWI-10442

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

AWI-10442 is a spare part associated with [WTG-11](../asset/wtg-11.md).[^c1][^c2][^c3][^c4] It is listed for a leaking hydraulic hose in the yaw brake circuit, and it is not stocked.[^c1][^c2][^c3][^c4]

## Facts

- Near the end of the export, WO-26-0444 for [WTG-05](../asset/wtg-05.md) is in progress for [rescue kit](../component/rescue-kit.md) inspection, WO-26-0450 for [WTG-11](../asset/wtg-11.md) is waiting for parts for a leaking hydraulic hose in the yaw brake circuit with part AWI-10442 quantity 1, and WO-26-0457 for [WTG-03](../asset/wtg-03.md) is open for yaw brake pad inspection.[^c1][^c2][^c3]
- The AWI-10442 hose for WTG-11 is not stocked and an order is to be raised.[^c4]
