# PWS-320-D208

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

PWS-320-D208 is a spare part identified as [Proseware PWG 320](../product/pwg-320.md) gear oil in a 208 L drum.[^c1] It is also specified as the new oil for [gearbox](../component/gearbox.md) oil changes, supplied as two drums per turbine for 410 L.[^c2] The current stock entry shows 1 drum on hand, with 0 on order.[^c1]

## Facts

- PWS-320-D208 is listed as [Proseware PWG 320](../product/pwg-320.md) gear oil in a 208 L drum with a do-not-use note tied to MOC-2025-012; the stock entry shows 1 drum on hand, minimum 0, reorder quantity 0, and 0 on order.[^c1]
- Superseded: The specified new oil is Proseware PWG 320 (ISO VG 320), with 410 L per turbine supplied as 2 drums PWS-320-D208.[^c2]
