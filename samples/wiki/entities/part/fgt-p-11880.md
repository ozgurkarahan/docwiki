# FGT-P-11880

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

FGT-P-11880 is a spare part used as a breather.[^c1] It must be replaced every 12 months.[^c1]

- The filter element [FGT-P-11872](fgt-p-11872.md) must be replaced every 12 months or at alarm [A2140](../alarm-code/a2140.md), and breather FGT-P-11880 must be replaced every 12 months.[^c1]
