# LWS-P-30422

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

LWS-P-30422 is a spare part referenced in the [LT-32](../product/lt-32.md) Maintenance Manual [gearbox](../component/gearbox.md) alarm guidance.[^c1] It is listed as a valve insert to check when alarm [A2101](../alarm-code/a2101.md) reports high gearbox oil sump temperature.[^c1]

## Facts

- Alarm [A2101](../alarm-code/a2101.md), Gearbox oil sump temperature high at 75 °C, can be caused by cooling, oil level, load/ambient-temperature, or bearing issues; actions include checking fan rotation and motor current, cleaning the radiator, checking valve insert LWS-P-30422, checking oil level, reviewing vibration trend, and taking an oil sample.[^c1]
