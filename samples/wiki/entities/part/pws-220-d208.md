# PWS-220-D208

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

PWS-220-D208 is a spare part used to supply [Proseware PWG 220](../product/pwg-220.md) for [gearbox](../component/gearbox.md) oil changes.[^c1] Each turbine requires 3 drums of PWS-220-D208 as part of a 470 L PWG 220 oil change.[^c1] It is also listed in a completed gearbox oil conversion campaign from [PWG 320](../product/pwg-320.md) to PWG 220.[^c2][^c3][^c4][^c5][^c6][^c7][^c8][^c9][^c10][^c11][^c12][^c13]

## Facts

- Action 2 is to order 36 drums of [PWG 220](../product/pwg-220.md) and set the minimum stock of PWS-220-D208 to 8 drums, owned by Tomasz Lebrecht and due 2025-08-15.[^c14]
- Each turbine requires 470 L of Proseware PWG 220, consisting of 60 L for flushing and 410 L for filling, supplied as 3 drums of PWS-220-D208.[^c1]
- The export records a completed high-priority modification campaign for [gearbox](../component/gearbox.md) oil conversion from [PWG 320](../product/pwg-320.md) to PWG 220 with a 60 L flush for [WTG-01](../asset/wtg-01.md) through [WTG-12](../asset/wtg-12.md) under reference MOC-2025-012; each row lists part PWS-220-D208 with quantity 3.[^c2][^c3][^c4][^c5][^c6][^c7][^c8][^c9][^c10][^c11][^c12][^c13]
