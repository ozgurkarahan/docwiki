# FGT-P-11872

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

FGT-P-11872 is an [oil filter element](../component/oil-filter.md) used in [LT-32](../product/lt-32.md) [gearbox](../component/gearbox.md) maintenance.[^c1][^c2][^c3][^c4][^c5] It is replaced during gearbox oil change and during conversion, and the filter housing cover screws are tightened to 85 Nm when it is replaced.[^c6][^c2][^c7][^c8] The part is scheduled for replacement every 12 months, or at alarm [A2140](../alarm-code/a2140.md).[^c2][^c9][^c5]

## Facts

- The [oil filter element](../component/oil-filter.md) FGT-P-11872 must be replaced during the conversion.[^c6]
- Per turbine, parts and labour include 470 L of [Proseware PWG 220](../product/pwg-220.md), one FGT-P-11872 oil filter element, one PWG 220 label kit, and labour by 2 technicians for about 6 hours.[^c1]
- The filter element FGT-P-11872 must be replaced every 12 months or at alarm [A2140](../alarm-code/a2140.md), and breather [FGT-P-11880](fgt-p-11880.md) must be replaced every 12 months.[^c2]
- During oil change, the filter element FGT-P-11872 is replaced, the magnetic plug is inspected, and filling is only through a filter cart with a 10 micron element.[^c2]
- Alarm A2140, Gearbox oil filter differential pressure high at 2.5 bar, can be caused by a clogged filter element or cold oil; the action is to replace filter element FGT-P-11872.[^c9]
- One oil filter element FGT-P-11872 is required.[^c3][^c4]
- The filter element FGT-P-11872 is replaced and the filter housing cover screws are tightened to 85 Nm.[^c7]
- [Gearbox](../component/gearbox.md) oil filter element replacement task MP-GBX-02 uses part FGT-P-11872, is scheduled every 12 months, and references [SOP-GBX-010](../procedure/sop-gbx-010.md).[^c5]
- The oil filter element FGT-P-11872 is to be replaced during the conversion.[^c8]
- After switching from [PWG 320](../product/pwg-320.md) to PWG 220, oil filter element FGT-P-11872 is to be changed a second time after 500 operating hours because loosened deposits collect in the filter.[^c10]
- Superseded: The FGT-P-11872 filter element is replaced, and the filter housing cover screws are tightened to 85 Nm.[^c11]
