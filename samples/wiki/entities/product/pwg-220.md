# Proseware PWG 220

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

Proseware PWG 220 is an ISO VG 220 [gearbox](../component/gearbox.md) oil used in the gearboxes of [Quellmoor Ridge](../asset/quellmoor-ridge-wind-farm.md) wind turbines.[^c1][^c2][^c3] It replaced [Proseware PWG 320](pwg-320.md) in the turbine gearboxes under [SB-LWS-2025-07](../bulletin/sb-lws-2025-07.md) and MOC-2025-012.[^c4][^c1][^c5][^c6][^c7][^c8][^c9][^c10][^c11][^c12][^c13][^c14][^c15][^c16][^c17] The oil-change procedure uses 60 L for flushing and 410 L for filling, and top-up is with PWG 220 only.[^c18][^c19][^c20][^c21][^c22][^c3]

## Facts

- For affected turbines, the gear oil changes from [Proseware PWG 320](pwg-320.md) to Proseware PWG 220.[^c4]
- The [gearbox](../component/gearbox.md) must be flushed with 60 L of PWG 220, the electric oil pump run for 30 minutes, and the flush oil then drained.[^c4]
- The gearbox must be filled with 410 L of PWG 220 through a filter cart with a 10 micron element.[^c4]
- The gearbox filler must be labelled 'PWG 220' using the supplied label kit.[^c4]
- Per turbine, parts and labour include 470 L of Proseware PWG 220, one [FGT-P-11872](../part/fgt-p-11872.md) [oil filter element](../component/oil-filter.md), one PWG 220 label kit, and labour by 2 technicians for about 6 hours.[^c23]
- The change replaces Proseware PWG 320 (ISO VG 320) with Proseware PWG 220 (ISO VG 220) in the gearboxes of all 12 turbines, as required by [SB-LWS-2025-07](../bulletin/sb-lws-2025-07.md) Rev. A.[^c1]
- Action 2 is to order 36 drums of PWG 220 and set the minimum stock of [PWS-220-D208](../part/pws-220-d208.md) to 8 drums, owned by Tomasz Lebrecht and due 2025-08-15.[^c24]
- The estimated cost is EUR 58,600 for 36 drums of PWG 220, 12 filter elements, labour, and waste-oil disposal, within the 2025 maintenance budget.[^c25]
- The lubricant is Proseware PWG 220, ISO VG 220, in service since 2025-10-15.[^c2]
- The report states that viscosity and water content are normal for PWG 220.[^c2]
- The trend table shows the 2026-06-10 sample as PWG 220 oil with Fe 64 ppm, PQ index 112, viscosity at 40 °C of 229, and ALERT status.[^c26]
- Each turbine requires 470 L of Proseware PWG 220, consisting of 60 L for flushing and 410 L for filling, supplied as 3 drums of PWS-220-D208.[^c18]
- Required tools include a torque wrench with a 20 to 300 Nm range, oil sample bottles, and a PWG 220 label kit.[^c27]
- The flush step uses 60 L of PWG 220, runs the electric oil pump for 30 minutes, and then drains the flush oil into waste containers.[^c19]
- The gearbox is filled with 410 L of Proseware PWG 220 through a 10 micron filter cart.[^c20]
- After 20 minutes of standstill, the oil level is checked at the sight glass according to [SPL-012](../procedure/spl-012.md) and topped up only with PWG 220.[^c21]
- Controller parameter GBX_TMIN_START must be set to 5 °C, and the gearbox filler must be labelled PWG 220.[^c28]
- Revision B changed the oil to PWG 220 per MOC-2025-012 and SB-LWS-2025-07, added the flush step, and set a minimum start-up temperature of +5 °C.[^c5]
- [WTG-09](../asset/wtg-09.md) has gearbox oil conversion to PWG 220 pending and deferred under SB-LWS-2025-07.[^c29]
- Gearbox oil change task MP-GBX-03 for PWG 220 is scheduled every 5 years or on condition, references [SOP-GBX-010](../procedure/sop-gbx-010.md) Rev. B, has a duration field of 6 h, and notes that the next planned changes start from 2030.[^c30]
- [WTG-07](../asset/wtg-07.md) oil sampling report TR-OA-2026-0614 recorded PWG 220 oil, Fe of 64 ppm, PQ index of 112, condition ALERT, and next sample due 2026-07-10 with resampling within 30 days.[^c31]
- Issue 1 was issued on 2025-12-15 and updated the oil sampling schedule for PWG 220 under MOC-2025-012 action 5.[^c32]
- Top-up is only permitted with PWG 220 through the 10 micron filter cart, and PWG 320 must not be used.[^c22]
- The export records a completed high-priority modification campaign for gearbox oil conversion from PWG 320 to PWG 220 with a 60 L flush for [WTG-01](../asset/wtg-01.md) through [WTG-12](../asset/wtg-12.md) under reference MOC-2025-012; each row lists part PWS-220-D208 with quantity 3.[^c6][^c7][^c8][^c9][^c10][^c11][^c12][^c13][^c14][^c15][^c16][^c17]
- Since SB-LWS-2025-07, the gearboxes use Proseware PWG 220 with ISO VG 220 instead of PWG 320.[^c3]
- Oils must not be mixed; top-up is with PWG 220 only.[^c3]
- The quiz answers state that the HSS drives the [generator](../component/generator.md), [A2102](../alarm-code/a2102.md) trips at 82 °C, the oil used is Proseware PWG 220, and a rising PQ index means more ferrous wear debris, often from a bearing.[^c33]
- For the conversion, the oil is to be changed from Proseware PWG 320 to Proseware PWG 220 at the next scheduled oil change, and no later than 31 December 2025.[^c34]
- Each gearbox is to be flushed with 60 L of PWG 220 before the final fill of 410 L.[^c35]
- After switching from PWG 320 to PWG 220, oil filter element FGT-P-11872 is to be changed a second time after 500 operating hours because loosened deposits collect in the filter.[^c36]
- PWG 220 conversion status was: nine turbines completed, [WTG-11](../asset/wtg-11.md) being converted that day, WTG-12 scheduled for the following Wednesday, and WTG-09 planned for the twenty-fourth if the access road remained open.[^c37][^c38]
- With PWG 220, the minimum oil temperature for start-up is plus five degrees; with the old PWG 320, it was plus ten.[^c39]
