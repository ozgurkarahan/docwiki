# Proseware PWG 320

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

Proseware PWG 320 is a gear oil with viscosity grade ISO VG 320 and synthetic PAO base oil.[^c1] It was the approved gear oil for the [LT-32](lt-32.md) [gearbox](../component/gearbox.md) before the conversion to [Proseware PWG 220](pwg-220.md).[^c1][^c2][^c3] Current procedures state that PWG 320 must not be used for topping up and that residual PWG 320 must remain below 5% after flushing.[^c4][^c5][^c6]

## Facts

- For affected turbines, the gear oil changes from Proseware PWG 320 to [Proseware PWG 220](pwg-220.md).[^c7]
- PWG 320 must be drained at an oil temperature between 40 and 60 °C.[^c7]
- After flushing, residual PWG 320 must not exceed 5 % of total oil volume.[^c7]
- The approved gear oil is Proseware PWG 320 with viscosity grade ISO VG 320 and synthetic PAO base oil.[^c1]
- The change replaces Proseware PWG 320 (ISO VG 320) with Proseware PWG 220 (ISO VG 220) in the gearboxes of all 12 turbines, as required by [SB-LWS-2025-07](../bulletin/sb-lws-2025-07.md) Rev. A.[^c2]
- For the oil-mixing hazard, the identified risk is residual PWG 320 above 5 %, controlled by a 60 L flush, labelling the filler, and removing PWG 320 drums from the shelf.[^c8]
- A converted [gearbox](../component/gearbox.md) must never be topped up with PWG 320, and residual PWG 320 must remain below 5 % of oil volume.[^c4]
- Top-up is only permitted with PWG 220 through the 10 micron filter cart, and PWG 320 must not be used.[^c9]
- [PWS-320-D208](../part/pws-320-d208.md) is listed as Proseware PWG 320 gear oil in a 208 L drum with a do-not-use note tied to MOC-2025-012; the stock entry shows 1 drum on hand, minimum 0, reorder quantity 0, and 0 on order.[^c10]
- The export records a completed high-priority modification campaign for gearbox oil conversion from PWG 320 to PWG 220 with a 60 L flush for [WTG-01](../asset/wtg-01.md) through [WTG-12](../asset/wtg-12.md) under reference MOC-2025-012; each row lists part [PWS-220-D208](../part/pws-220-d208.md) with quantity 3.[^c11][^c12][^c13][^c14][^c15][^c16][^c17][^c18][^c19][^c20][^c21][^c22]
- Since SB-LWS-2025-07, the gearboxes use Proseware PWG 220 with ISO VG 220 instead of PWG 320.[^c3]
- The minimum oil temperature for start-up is +5 °C, compared with +10 °C when using PWG 320.[^c3]
- For the conversion, the oil is to be changed from Proseware PWG 320 to Proseware PWG 220 at the next scheduled oil change, and no later than 31 December 2025.[^c23]
- Residual PWG 320 above 5 % after flushing is not permitted.[^c5]
- After switching from PWG 320 to PWG 220, [oil filter element](../component/oil-filter.md) [FGT-P-11872](../part/fgt-p-11872.md) is to be changed a second time after 500 operating hours because loosened deposits collect in the filter.[^c24]
- With PWG 220, the minimum oil temperature for start-up is plus five degrees; with the old PWG 320, it was plus ten.[^c25]
- If [WTG-09](../asset/wtg-09.md) still has PWG 320 in January, its start temperature remains plus ten until conversion is complete; the parameter is changed only with the new oil.[^c26]
- After conversion, a gearbox must never be topped up with PWG 320; old PWG 320 drums are marked do not use.[^c6]
- One drum of PWG 320 remained in the store and was to stay there until the waste carrier collected it; it was not to be used.[^c27][^c28]
- Superseded: The specified new oil is Proseware PWG 320 (ISO VG 320), with 410 L per turbine supplied as 2 drums PWS-320-D208.[^c29]
- Superseded: 410 L of Proseware PWG 320 is filled through a 10 micron filter cart.[^c30]
- Superseded: Revision A is the first issue and is associated with PWG 320.[^c31]

## Timeline

- 2025-07: Since [SB-LWS-2025-07](../bulletin/sb-lws-2025-07.md), the gearboxes use [Proseware PWG 220](pwg-220.md) with ISO VG 220 instead of PWG 320.[^c3]
- 2025-07: The change replaces Proseware PWG 320 (ISO VG 320) with Proseware PWG 220 (ISO VG 220) in the gearboxes of all 12 turbines, as required by SB-LWS-2025-07 Rev. A.[^c2]
- 2025-12-31: For the conversion, the oil is to be changed from Proseware PWG 320 to Proseware PWG 220 at the next scheduled oil change, and no later than 31 December 2025.[^c23]
