# Fabrikam FGT-3200

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

The Fabrikam FGT-3200 is a three-stage [gearbox](../component/gearbox.md) used on [Litware LT-32](lt-32.md) turbines, with one [planetary stage](../component/planetary-stage.md) followed by two parallel helical stages.[^c1][^c2] Its rated input torque is 2,410 kNm and its dry mass is 21.6 t.[^c2] Only the lubricant listed in Table 4-2 is approved for the FGT-3200 gearbox, and lubricants of different brands or viscosity grades must not be mixed.[^c3]

## Facts

- The bulletin applies to [LT-32](lt-32.md) turbines delivered in CCV configuration with Fabrikam FGT-3200 gearboxes and to LT-32 standard turbines at sites where ambient temperature regularly falls below -20 °C.[^c4]
- The document applies to LT-32 turbines of all variants with the Fabrikam FGT-3200 [gearbox](../component/gearbox.md).[^c1]
- The LT-32 uses a Fabrikam FGT-3200 three-stage gearbox with one [planetary stage](../component/planetary-stage.md) followed by two parallel helical stages.[^c2]
- The FGT-3200 gearbox rated input torque is 2,410 kNm and its dry mass is 21.6 t.[^c2]
- Only the lubricant listed in Table 4-2 is approved for the FGT-3200 gearbox, and lubricants of different brands or viscosity grades must not be mixed.[^c3]
- The asset is the [WTG-07](../asset/wtg-07.md) main gearbox, a Fabrikam FGT-3200 with serial FGT-3200-18-07752.[^c5]
- The procedure applies to [WTG-01](../asset/wtg-01.md) to [WTG-12](../asset/wtg-12.md) with Litware LT-32 CCV turbines and Fabrikam FGT-3200 gearboxes.[^c6]
- The incident concerns WTG-07, serial LT32-19-0421, with gearbox FGT-3200-18-07752.[^c7]
- All turbine entries are Litware LT-32 CCV turbines with Fabrikam FGT-3200 gearboxes, Hub height (m) 92, and Rotor diameter (m) 118.[^c8][^c9][^c10][^c11][^c12][^c13][^c14][^c15][^c16][^c17][^c18][^c19]
- [FGT-P-22328](../part/fgt-p-22328.md), the [HSS bearing](../component/hss-bearing.md) on the [generator](../component/generator.md) side for FGT-3200, is a critical item below minimum stock: 0 pc on hand, minimum 1 pc, reorder quantity 2 pc, and 2 pc on order under PO-76994 for expected delivery on 2026-09-25.[^c20][^c21]
- The deck is about the Fabrikam FGT-3200 in the Litware LT-32 and is dated October 2025.[^c22]
- The configuration records checked for [Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm](../asset/quellmoor-ridge-wind-farm.md) showed that all 12 turbines, WTG-01 through WTG-12, were delivered as LT-32 in CCV configuration with Fabrikam FGT-3200 gearboxes, so [SB-LWS-2025-07](../bulletin/sb-lws-2025-07.md) applies to all 12 turbines.[^c23]
- Superseded: The procedure applies to WTG-01 to WTG-12, specified as Litware LT-32 CCV turbines with Fabrikam FGT-3200 gearboxes.[^c24]
