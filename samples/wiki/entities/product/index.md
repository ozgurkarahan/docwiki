- [Proseware PWG 220](pwg-220.md): Proseware PWG 220 is an ISO VG 220 gearbox oil used in the gearboxes of Quellmoor Ridge wind turbines. It replaced Proseware PWG 320 in the turbine gearboxes under SB-LWS-2025-07 and MOC-2025-012. The oil-change procedure uses 60 L for flushing and 410 L for filling, and top-up is with PWG 220 only.

It was the approved gear oil for the LT-32 gearbox before the conversion to Proseware PWG 220.
