# Litware LT-32

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

Litware LT-32 is a wind turbine product that uses a [Fabrikam FGT-3200](fgt-3200.md) three-stage [gearbox](../component/gearbox.md) with one [planetary stage](../component/planetary-stage.md) followed by two parallel helical stages.[^c1] At [Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm](../asset/quellmoor-ridge-wind-farm.md), 12 Litware LT-32 CCV turbines are recorded, with turbine IDs [WTG-01](../asset/wtg-01.md) to [WTG-12](../asset/wtg-12.md).[^c2][^c3][^c4][^c5][^c6][^c7][^c8][^c9][^c10][^c11][^c12][^c13][^c14][^c15] The LT-32 has listed lockout/tagout isolation points for electrical, hydraulic, rotational, and thermal energy sources.[^c16][^c17][^c18][^c19][^c20][^c21]

## Facts

- The bulletin applies to LT-32 turbines delivered in CCV configuration with [Fabrikam FGT-3200](fgt-3200.md) gearboxes and to LT-32 standard turbines at sites where ambient temperature regularly falls below -20 °C.[^c22]
- The bulletin applies to all variants of LT-32 turbines with M30 10.9 [pitch bearing bolts](../component/pitch-bearing-bolts.md), 72 bolts per blade bearing, and 216 bolts per turbine.[^c23]
- The reason for the bulletin is that loose pitch bearing bolts were found on two LT-32 turbines at another site after three years of operation, and a one-time re-torque restores the preload.[^c23]
- The document applies to LT-32 turbines of all variants with the Fabrikam FGT-3200 [gearbox](../component/gearbox.md).[^c24]
- The LT-32 uses a Fabrikam FGT-3200 three-stage gearbox with one [planetary stage](../component/planetary-stage.md) followed by two parallel helical stages.[^c1]
- The report covers [Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm](../asset/quellmoor-ridge-wind-farm.md), which is recorded as having 12 Litware LT-32 turbines and 36 blades of 57.5 m.[^c2]
- The procedure applies to [WTG-01](../asset/wtg-01.md) to [WTG-12](../asset/wtg-12.md) with Litware LT-32 CCV turbines and Fabrikam FGT-3200 gearboxes.[^c25]
- For LT-32 electrical 690 V energy from the [generator](../component/generator.md), converter, and auxiliaries is isolated at the main circuit breaker in the tower base.[^c16]
- For LT-32 electrical 33 kV energy from the transformer feeder is isolated at the ring main unit, which is for authorised persons only.[^c17]
- For LT-32 controller uninterruptible power, the source is the UPS of the controller and the isolation point is the UPS output breaker.[^c18]
- For LT-32 hydraulic energy from pitch and yaw brake accumulators, the isolation point is relief valves to 0 bar.[^c19]
- For LT-32 rotational energy from the rotor and drivetrain, the isolation point is the rotor lock pins.[^c20]
- For LT-32 thermal energy from hot gear oil and heaters, the isolation point is cool-down and the heater breaker.[^c21]
- The instruction describes checking and replacing the [generator brushes](../component/generator-brushes.md) of Litware LT-32 wind turbines at the Quellmoor Ridge wind farm.[^c26]
- All turbine entries are Litware LT-32 CCV turbines with Fabrikam FGT-3200 gearboxes, Hub height (m) 92, and Rotor diameter (m) 118.[^c3][^c4][^c5][^c6][^c7][^c8][^c9][^c10][^c11][^c12][^c13][^c14]
- The deck is about the Fabrikam FGT-3200 in the Litware LT-32 and is dated October 2025.[^c27]
- Quellmoor Ridge has 12 Litware LT-32 turbines in CCV configuration, identified WTG-01 to WTG-12, with total capacity of 38.4 MW.[^c15]
- The configuration records checked for Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm showed that all 12 turbines, WTG-01 through WTG-12, were delivered as LT-32 in CCV configuration with Fabrikam FGT-3200 gearboxes, so [SB-LWS-2025-07](../bulletin/sb-lws-2025-07.md) applies to all 12 turbines.[^c28]
- The original requester stated that [Contoso](../organisation/contoso-renewables.md) operates 12 LT-32 turbines at Quellmoor Ridge and reported that on 21 January two turbines needed more than 6 hours of heating before start-up at -24.6 degrees C.[^c29]
- Superseded: The procedure applies to WTG-01 to WTG-12, specified as Litware LT-32 CCV turbines with Fabrikam FGT-3200 gearboxes.[^c30]
