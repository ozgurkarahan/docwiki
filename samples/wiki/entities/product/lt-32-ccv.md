# LT-32 CCV

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

LT-32 CCV is a [Litware](../organisation/litware-wind-systems.md) turbine model used with [Fabrikam FGT-3200](fgt-3200.md) gearboxes on [WTG-01](../asset/wtg-01.md) to [WTG-12](../asset/wtg-12.md).[^c1]

## Facts

- The procedure applies to [WTG-01](../asset/wtg-01.md) to [WTG-12](../asset/wtg-12.md) with [Litware LT-32](lt-32.md) CCV turbines and [Fabrikam FGT-3200](fgt-3200.md) gearboxes.[^c1]
- Superseded: The procedure applies to WTG-01 to WTG-12, specified as Litware LT-32 CCV turbines with Fabrikam FGT-3200 gearboxes.[^c2]
