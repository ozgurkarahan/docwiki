# SB-LWS-2025-07 Gearbox oil viscosity grade change for LT-32 CCV turbines

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

SB-LWS-2025-07 is a service bulletin for [LT-32 CCV](../product/lt-32-ccv.md) turbines covering [gearbox](../component/gearbox.md) oil viscosity grade.[^c1][^c2] It changes the gearbox oil from [Proseware PWG 320](../product/pwg-320.md) (ISO VG 320) to [Proseware PWG 220](../product/pwg-220.md) (ISO VG 220).[^c3][^c4] The bulletin applies to all 12 CCV turbines at [Quellmoor Ridge](../asset/quellmoor-ridge-wind-farm.md).[^c3][^c2]

## Facts

- The change replaces [Proseware PWG 320](../product/pwg-320.md) (ISO VG 320) with [Proseware PWG 220](../product/pwg-220.md) (ISO VG 220) in the gearboxes of all 12 turbines, as required by SB-LWS-2025-07 Rev. A.[^c3]
- [Litware](../organisation/litware-wind-systems.md) support confirmed on 2025-05-20 under ticket LWS-TS-48213 that SB-LWS-2025-07 applies to all 12 CCV turbines of [Quellmoor Ridge](../asset/quellmoor-ridge-wind-farm.md).[^c3]
- Revision B supersedes [SOP-GBX-010](../procedure/sop-gbx-010.md) Rev. A and implements MOC-2025-012 and SB-LWS-2025-07 Rev. A.[^c5]
- Revision B changed the oil to PWG 220 per MOC-2025-012 and SB-LWS-2025-07, added the flush step, and set a minimum start-up temperature of +5 °C.[^c6]
- [WTG-09](../asset/wtg-09.md) has [gearbox](../component/gearbox.md) oil conversion to PWG 220 pending and deferred under SB-LWS-2025-07.[^c7]
- Since SB-LWS-2025-07, the gearboxes use Proseware PWG 220 with ISO VG 220 instead of PWG 320.[^c4]
- The email was sent on 20 May 2025 with the subject “RE: [LWS-TS-48213] Applicability of SB-LWS-2025-07 to Quellmoor Ridge.”[^c8]
- Litware’s response addresses a 29 April 2025 question about SB-LWS-2025-07 Rev. A, described as covering gearbox oil viscosity grade for CCV turbines.[^c1]
- The configuration records checked for Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm showed that all 12 turbines, [WTG-01](../asset/wtg-01.md) through [WTG-12](../asset/wtg-12.md), were delivered as [LT-32](../product/lt-32.md) in CCV configuration with [Fabrikam FGT-3200](../product/fgt-3200.md) gearboxes, so SB-LWS-2025-07 applies to all 12 turbines.[^c2]
- The original requester asked whether SB-LWS-2025-07 applies to all Quellmoor Ridge turbines and whether the conversion could be done during the autumn maintenance campaign.[^c9]
