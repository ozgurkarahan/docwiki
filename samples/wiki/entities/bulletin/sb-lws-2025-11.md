# SB-LWS-2025-11 Pitch bearing bolt re-torque

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

SB-LWS-2025-11 is service bulletin number SB-LWS-2025-11, revision A, titled [Pitch bearing](../component/pitch-bearing.md) bolt re-torque.[^c1] The maintenance plan for [Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm](../asset/quellmoor-ridge-wind-farm.md) includes pitch bearing bolt re-torque task MP-PIT-01, covering 100% of bolts at 1,450 Nm every 12 months, and referencing SB-LWS-2025-11 Rev. A.[^c2] Work orders through 2026-08-31 show pitch bearing bolt re-torque work completed for [WTG-01](../asset/wtg-01.md) through [WTG-06](../asset/wtg-06.md) and planned for [WTG-07](../asset/wtg-07.md) through [WTG-12](../asset/wtg-12.md), with 1,450 Nm and 100% of bolts, referencing SB-LWS-2025-11 Rev. A.[^c3][^c4][^c5][^c6][^c7][^c8][^c9][^c10][^c11][^c12][^c13][^c14]

## Facts

- The bulletin number is SB-LWS-2025-11 and the revision is A.[^c1]
- [Pitch bearing](../component/pitch-bearing.md) bolt re-torque task MP-PIT-01 covers 100 % of bolts at 1,450 Nm, is scheduled every 12 months, and references SB-LWS-2025-11 Rev. A.[^c2]
- Pitch bearing bolt re-torque work for [WTG-01](../asset/wtg-01.md) through [WTG-06](../asset/wtg-06.md) was completed with a stated torque of 1,450 Nm and coverage of 100 % of bolts, referenced to SB-LWS-2025-11 Rev. A.[^c3][^c4][^c5][^c6][^c7][^c8]
- Pitch bearing bolt re-torque work for [WTG-07](../asset/wtg-07.md) through [WTG-12](../asset/wtg-12.md) is planned for September 2026, with each title stating 1,450 Nm and 100 % of bolts and reference SB-LWS-2025-11 Rev. A.[^c9][^c10][^c11][^c12][^c13][^c14]
