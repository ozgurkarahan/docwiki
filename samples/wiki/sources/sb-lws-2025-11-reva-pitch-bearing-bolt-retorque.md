# SB-LWS-2025-11 Rev. A - Pitch Bearing Bolt Re-torque

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

This [Litware Wind Systems](../entities/organisation/litware-wind-systems.md) service bulletin covers a mandatory one-time re-torque of [pitch bearing bolts](../entities/component/pitch-bearing-bolts.md) on [LT-32](../entities/product/lt-32.md) turbines. It states the reason, required torqueing instructions, compliance timing, and record-return requirements.

## Key facts

- [Litware Wind Systems](../entities/organisation/litware-wind-systems.md) issued the service bulletin on 2025-10-06.[^c1]
- The bulletin number is [SB-LWS-2025-11](../entities/bulletin/sb-lws-2025-11.md) and the revision is A.[^c1]
- The bulletin category is mandatory.[^c1]
- The bulletin applies to all variants of [LT-32](../entities/product/lt-32.md) turbines with M30 10.9 [pitch bearing bolts](../entities/component/pitch-bearing-bolts.md), 72 bolts per blade bearing, and 216 bolts per turbine.[^c1]
- Compliance is required at the next 12-month service.[^c1]
- The reason for the bulletin is that loose pitch bearing bolts were found on two LT-32 turbines at another site after three years of operation, and a one-time re-torque restores the preload.[^c1]
- The turbine must be stopped, the rotor locked, and the pitch system isolated before the work.[^c1]
- The hydraulic torque wrench used for the work must have been calibrated within the last 12 months and have accuracy of +/- 3 %.[^c1]
- Technicians must re-torque 100 % of the pitch bearing bolts to 1,450 Nm in the cross pattern of the bolt map, blade by blade.[^c1]
- Each bolt must be marked with a paint line after re-torque.[^c1]
- Every bolt that turns more than 60 degrees before reaching the torque must be recorded and reported to Litware Wind Systems.[^c1]
- The turbine, blade, torque wrench serial number, and bolts that turned must be recorded on the re-torque form, which must be returned to Litware Wind Systems within 60 days.[^c1]

### Reason

Loose [pitch bearing bolts](../entities/component/pitch-bearing-bolts.md) were found on two [LT-32](../entities/product/lt-32.md) turbines at another site after three years of operation.[^c2]

The bulletin states that a one-time re-torque of all bolts restores the preload.[^c2]

### Instructions

- Stop the turbine, lock the rotor, and isolate the pitch system.[^c2]
- Use a hydraulic torque wrench calibrated within the last 12 months with accuracy of +/- 3 %.[^c2]
- Re-torque 100 % of the [pitch bearing bolts](../entities/component/pitch-bearing-bolts.md) to 1,450 Nm in the cross pattern of the bolt map, blade by blade.[^c2]
- Mark each bolt with a paint line after re-torque.[^c2]
- Record every bolt that turns more than 60 degrees before reaching the torque and report it to [Litware Wind Systems](../entities/organisation/litware-wind-systems.md).[^c2]

### Records

Record the turbine, blade, torque wrench serial number, and the bolts that turned on the re-torque form, and return the form to [Litware Wind Systems](../entities/organisation/litware-wind-systems.md) within 60 days.[^c2]
