# Shift Handover for 2026-07-29 Day Shift

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

This is a fictional day-shift handover note dated 2026-07-29 by Callum Rydings. It records turbine maintenance findings, parts and work-order status, monitoring actions, and expected night curtailment.

## Key facts

- The document is marked as fictional and not for operational use, and it is a day-shift handover for 2026-07-29 by Callum Rydings.[^c1]
- [WTG-02](../entities/asset/wtg-02.md) had an endoscope inspection on 23/07 that found spalling on the [HSS bearing](../entities/component/hss-bearing.md) on the [generator](../entities/component/generator.md) side.[^c2]
- WO-26-0439 for WTG-02 was raised on 24/07 and is waiting for the same bearing as [WTG-07](../entities/asset/wtg-07.md), part [FGT-P-22328](../entities/part/fgt-p-22328.md).[^c2]
- WTG-02 is kept in operation and its vibration is reviewed weekly.[^c2]
- [WTG-11](../entities/asset/wtg-11.md) had a small hydraulic oil leak at a yaw brake hose found on the day of the handover.[^c2]
- The [AWI-10442](../entities/part/awi-10442.md) hose for WTG-11 is not stocked and an order is to be raised.[^c2]
- The WTG-11 leak was contained with absorbent pads and is checked every shift.[^c2]
- [WTG-05](../entities/asset/wtg-05.md) [rescue kit](../entities/component/rescue-kit.md) inspection WO-26-0444 has started and is to finish next week.[^c2]
- WTG-07 has had no oil temperature alarm since 24/06.[^c2]
- [WTG-03](../entities/asset/wtg-03.md), [WTG-04](../entities/asset/wtg-04.md), and WTG-05 are expected to have bat curtailment again at night due to low wind.[^c2]
