# Fabrikam Supplier Note on PO-76994 HSS Bearings and PWG 220 Follow-up

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

This is a [Fabrikam](../entities/organisation/fabrikam-getriebetechnik.md) Getriebetechnik supplier note to [Contoso Renewables](../entities/organisation/contoso-renewables.md) for [Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm](../entities/asset/quellmoor-ridge-wind-farm.md). It covers a delayed delivery of HSS bearings [FGT-P-22328](../entities/part/fgt-p-22328.md), follow-up after switching from [PWG 320](../entities/product/pwg-320.md) to [PWG 220](../entities/product/pwg-220.md), and installation requirements for the HSS bearings.

## Key facts

- The document is marked fictional and not for operational use.[^c1]
- The supplier note is dated 03.08.2026 and is addressed to [Contoso Renewables](../entities/organisation/contoso-renewables.md) at [Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm](../entities/asset/quellmoor-ridge-wind-farm.md), with the subject PO-76994 for HSS bearings [FGT-P-22328](../entities/part/fgt-p-22328.md) and notes after the oil changeover.[^c2]
- The two HSS bearings FGT-P-22328 from PO-76994 cannot be delivered in calendar week 39.[^c3]
- The new confirmed delivery window is calendar week 40, from 28.09.2026 to 02.10.2026.[^c3]
- The stated delivery-delay reason is a delay in heat treatment of the outer rings, and air freight would no longer shorten the delivery time.[^c3]
- After switching from [PWG 320](../entities/product/pwg-320.md) to [PWG 220](../entities/product/pwg-220.md), [oil filter element](../entities/component/oil-filter.md) [FGT-P-11872](../entities/part/fgt-p-11872.md) is to be changed a second time after 500 operating hours because loosened deposits collect in the filter.[^c4]
- At the same time as the second oil filter element change, the supplier recommends an oil sample.[^c4]
- Before installation, the new bearing is to be heated inductively to 110 °C, with a maximum of 120 °C.[^c5]
- The tightening torque for the M16 bearing cover screws is 210 Nm.[^c5]
- After installing the HSS bearings, an oil sample is to be taken after 500 operating hours.[^c5]

## Contents

### Header and correspondence metadata

The document is marked “FICTIONAL - NOT FOR OPERATIONAL USE.”[^c2]

It is a [Fabrikam Getriebetechnik GmbH](../entities/organisation/fabrikam-getriebetechnik.md) supplier note dated 03.08.2026, addressed to [Contoso Renewables](../entities/organisation/contoso-renewables.md) at [Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm](../entities/asset/quellmoor-ridge-wind-farm.md) and sent by Katrin Ostermeier from Field Service.[^c2]

The subject is PO-76994 for [HSS bearing](../entities/component/hss-bearing.md) [FGT-P-22328](../entities/part/fgt-p-22328.md) and notes after the oil changeover.[^c2]

### Delivery date

The two HSS bearings [FGT-P-22328](../entities/part/fgt-p-22328.md) from PO-76994 cannot be delivered in calendar week 39.[^c6]

The new confirmed delivery date is calendar week 40, from 28.09.2026 to 02.10.2026.[^c6]

The stated reason is a delay in heat treatment of the outer rings, and air freight would no longer shorten the delivery time.[^c6]

### Note after the switch to PWG 220

After switching from [PWG 320](../entities/product/pwg-320.md) to [PWG 220](../entities/product/pwg-220.md), [oil filter element](../entities/component/oil-filter.md) [FGT-P-11872](../entities/part/fgt-p-11872.md) is to be changed a second time after 500 operating hours because loosened deposits collect in the filter.[^c7]

At the same time, the supplier recommends taking an oil sample.[^c7]

### Installation of the HSS bearings

Before installation, the new bearing is to be heated inductively to 110 °C, with a maximum of 120 °C.[^c8]

The tightening torque for the M16 bearing cover screws is 210 Nm.[^c8]

After installation, an oil sample is to be taken after 500 operating hours.[^c8]

### Closing

The sender states that she is available for questions.[^c9]

The note is signed by Katrin Ostermeier, Field Service Engineer at [Fabrikam Getriebetechnik GmbH](../entities/organisation/fabrikam-getriebetechnik.md), and ends with the same fictional-use disclaimer.[^c10]
