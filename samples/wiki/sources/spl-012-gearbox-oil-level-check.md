# SPL-012 Gearbox Oil Level Check

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

This source is a single-point lesson for [Quellmoor Ridge](../entities/asset/quellmoor-ridge-wind-farm.md), dated 2025-09-29, on checking [gearbox](../entities/component/gearbox.md) oil level. It covers stopping and waiting before reading the sight glass, acceptable MIN/MAX level criteria, top-up restrictions, checklist recording, and the action required if the level is below MIN.

## Key facts

- [SPL-012](../entities/procedure/spl-012.md) is titled "[Gearbox](../entities/component/gearbox.md) oil level check."[^c1]
- The document is a single-point lesson for [Quellmoor Ridge](../entities/asset/quellmoor-ridge-wind-farm.md) dated 2025-09-29 by Callum Rydings.[^c1]
- The lesson is marked fictional and not for operational use.[^c1]
- To check the gearbox oil level, stop the turbine and wait 20 minutes so the oil settles.[^c2]
- Read the sight glass on the [generator](../entities/component/generator.md) side of the gearbox.[^c2]
- The gearbox oil level must be between MIN and MAX.[^c2]
- Top-up is only permitted with [PWG 220](../entities/product/pwg-220.md) through the 10 micron filter cart, and [PWG 320](../entities/product/pwg-320.md) must not be used.[^c2]
- Record the gearbox oil level check result on item 4 of the daily checklist.[^c2]
- If the level is below MIN, do not restart the turbine and report it to the O&M Supervisor because of the risk of alarm [A2130](../entities/alarm-code/a2130.md).[^c3]

### How to check

Stop the turbine and wait 20 minutes so the oil settles.[^c5] Read the sight glass on the [generator](../entities/component/generator.md) side of the [gearbox](../entities/component/gearbox.md).[^c5] The oil level must be between MIN and MAX.[^c5] Top up only with [PWG 220](../entities/product/pwg-220.md) through the 10 micron filter cart, and never use [PWG 320](../entities/product/pwg-320.md).[^c5] Record the result on item 4 of the daily checklist.[^c5]

### Good or not good

The lesson asks the operator to judge whether the level is good or not good.[^c6] If the level is below MIN, do not restart; report it to the O&M Supervisor because of the risk of alarm [A2130](../entities/alarm-code/a2130.md).[^c6]
