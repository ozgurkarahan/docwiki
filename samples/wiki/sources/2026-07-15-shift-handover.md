# Shift handover - 2026-07-15 (day shift to night shift)

## Key facts

- The document is marked fictional and not for operational use.[^c1]
- The handover is for 2026-07-15, from day shift to night shift.[^c2]
- Callum Rydings, Lead Wind Technician, wrote the handover note.[^c3]
- The site covered by the handover is [Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm](../entities/asset/quellmoor-ridge-wind-farm.md).[^c4]

- [WTG-03](../entities/asset/wtg-03.md), [WTG-04](../entities/asset/wtg-04.md) and [WTG-05](../entities/asset/wtg-05.md) are expected to stop from 22:00 if wind remains low due to bat curtailment, and this should not be reset.[^c5]
- [WTG-07](../entities/asset/wtg-07.md) still has [HSS bearing](../entities/component/hss-bearing.md) vibration around 5.3 mm/s; oil temperature has been normal since the fan motor replacement, and WO-26-0412 is waiting for the bearing.[^c5]
- On WTG-04, the 2026-07-02 drone check of blade B found the crack unchanged at 0.9 m.[^c5]
- The WTG-05 daily checklist CHK-05 was completed yesterday with all items OK.[^c6]
- [Rescue kit](../entities/component/rescue-kit.md) inspections were performed on [WTG-01](../entities/asset/wtg-01.md) to WTG-04 under MP-HSE-01.[^c6]
- Radiator cleaning MP-GBX-07 on WTG-03 was postponed to next week.[^c6]
- The next shift should watch WTG-07 oil temperature in the afternoon.[^c7]
- The spill kit of [WTG-12](../entities/asset/wtg-12.md) needs a refill, identified with [AWI-20915](../entities/part/awi-20915.md).[^c7]
- The document repeats the fictional, non-operational-use marking at the end.[^c8]
