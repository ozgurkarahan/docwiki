# Environmental Permit EP-2018-0417 - Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm (consolidated with variation 2)

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

This is the consolidated environmental permit for [Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm](../entities/asset/quellmoor-ridge-wind-farm.md), incorporating variation 1 and variation 2. It covers general permit conditions, noise and shadow flicker limits, ecology and bat curtailment, ice precautions, pollution prevention, reporting, and authority contacts.

## Key facts

- Environmental permit EP-2018-0417 applies to [Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm](../entities/asset/quellmoor-ridge-wind-farm.md) and is a consolidated version incorporating variation 1 and variation 2.[^c1]
- The permit holder is [Contoso Renewables](../entities/organisation/contoso-renewables.md).[^c1]
- The installation is Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm, comprising 12 wind turbines [WTG-01](../entities/asset/wtg-01.md) to [WTG-12](../entities/asset/wtg-12.md) with a capacity of 38.4 MW.[^c1]
- The original permit issue date was 2018-11-20, variation 1 was for automatic shadow flicker control, and variation 2 was for bat protection curtailment under condition 7.3.[^c1]
- The permit was issued by the [Skelvane District Environmental Authority](../entities/organisation/skelvane-district-environmental-authority.md).[^c1]
- The installation must be operated as described in the permit application.[^c1]
- The permit holder must notify the Authority before any change is made to the number or type of turbines.[^c1]
- A copy of the permit must be kept at the site.[^c1]
- Noise from the wind farm at dwellings of Quellmoor must not exceed 40 dB(A) LA90, 10 minutes at night between 23:00 and 07:00, and must not exceed 45 dB(A) during the day, at hub-height wind speeds up to 12 m/s.[^c1]
- Shadow flicker at any dwelling must not exceed 30 hours per year and 30 minutes per day, and automatic shadow flicker control must be used.[^c1]
- Bat activity must be monitored at the nacelle of [WTG-04](../entities/asset/wtg-04.md) with an acoustic detector from April to October each year.[^c2]
- Monitoring results must be reported in the annual environmental report.[^c2]
- From 1 June to 31 August each year, [WTG-03](../entities/asset/wtg-03.md), WTG-04 and [WTG-05](../entities/asset/wtg-05.md) must be stopped between 22:00 and 05:00 local time whenever hub-height wind speed is below 6.0 m/s and air temperature is 10 °C or higher.[^c2]
- Bat protection curtailment must be implemented automatically by the turbine control system and each curtailment period must be recorded in the turbine event log.[^c2]
- Curtailment records must be kept for 5 years and summarised in an annual report submitted to the Authority by 31 January of the following year.[^c2]
- The Authority may adjust the thresholds of condition 7.3 after three years of monitoring data.[^c2]
- Variation 2 was justified by the presence of a protected bat species maternity roost in Skelvane Wood, east of WTG-03, WTG-04 and WTG-05, with foraging near the turbines on warm, calm summer nights.[^c2]
- Turbines must stop automatically when ice is detected on rotor blades and may restart only when the ice has gone.[^c2]
- Warning signs about falling ice must be placed at all access points at a distance of 300 m from the turbines from November to March.[^c2]
- A spill kit must be kept in each turbine and in the O&M building.[^c2]
- Oil spills of more than 20 L outside a turbine must be reported to the Authority within 24 hours.[^c2]
- Oil drums must be stored in a bunded area with capacity of at least 110 % of the largest container or 25 % of total stored volume, whichever is larger.[^c2]
- Waste oil must be collected by a licensed carrier.[^c2]
- The annual environmental report must be submitted by 31 January.[^c2]
- Incidents with an effect on the environment must be notified within 24 hours.[^c2]
- The Authority contact is the Permitting Officer for the Skelvane district, and the permit is signed for the Skelvane District Environmental Authority by Siobhan Carrick, Permitting Officer, on 2024-12-10.[^c2]

### Part 1 - General conditions

- The installation must be operated as described in the permit application.[^c3]
- The permit holder must notify the Authority of any change to the number or type of turbines before the change is made.[^c3]
- A copy of the permit must be kept at the site.[^c3]

### Part 6 - Noise and shadow flicker

- Noise from the wind farm at the dwellings of Quellmoor must not exceed 40 dB(A) (LA90, 10 minutes) at night between 23:00 and 07:00 and 45 dB(A) during the day, at hub-height wind speeds up to 12 m/s.[^c3]
- Shadow flicker at any dwelling must not exceed 30 hours per year and 30 minutes per day.[^c3]
- Automatic shadow flicker control must be used.[^c3]

### Part 7 - Ecology

- Bat activity must be monitored at the nacelle of [WTG-04](../entities/asset/wtg-04.md) with an acoustic detector from April to October each year.[^c4]
- Monitoring results must be reported in the annual environmental report.[^c4]
- From 1 June to 31 August each year, [WTG-03](../entities/asset/wtg-03.md), WTG-04 and [WTG-05](../entities/asset/wtg-05.md) must be stopped between 22:00 and 05:00 local time whenever hub-height wind speed is below 6.0 m/s and air temperature is 10 °C or higher.[^c4]
- The curtailment must be implemented automatically by the turbine control system, and each curtailment period must be recorded in the turbine event log.[^c4]
- Curtailment records must be kept for 5 years and summarised in an annual report submitted to the Authority by 31 January of the following year.[^c4]
- The Authority may adjust the thresholds of condition 7.3 after three years of monitoring data.[^c4]
- Variation 2 is linked to Skelvane Wood, east of WTG-03, WTG-04 and WTG-05, which holds a maternity roost of a protected bat species that forages near the turbines on warm, calm summer nights.[^c4]

### Part 8 - Ice

- Turbines must stop automatically when ice is detected on the rotor blades and restart only when the ice has gone.[^c4]
- Warning signs about falling ice must be placed at all access points at a distance of 300 m from the turbines from November to March.[^c4]

### Part 9 - Pollution prevention

- A spill kit must be kept in each turbine and in the O&M building.[^c4]
- Oil spills of more than 20 L outside a turbine must be reported to the Authority within 24 hours.[^c4]
- Oil drums must be stored in a bunded area with capacity of at least 110 % of the largest container or 25 % of the total stored volume, whichever is larger.[^c4]
- Waste oil must be collected by a licensed carrier.[^c4]

### Part 10 - Reporting and contacts

- The annual environmental report must be submitted by 31 January.[^c4]
- Incidents with an effect on the environment must be notified within 24 hours.[^c4]
- The Authority contact is the Permitting Officer for the Skelvane district.[^c4]
- The permit is signed for the [Skelvane District Environmental Authority](../entities/organisation/skelvane-district-environmental-authority.md) by Siobhan Carrick, Permitting Officer, on 2024-12-10.[^c4]
