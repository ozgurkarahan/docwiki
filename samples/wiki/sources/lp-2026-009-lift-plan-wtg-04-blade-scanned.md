# Lift Plan LP-2026-009 - WTG-04 Blade B Replacement

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

This is a [Tailspin Heavy Lift](../entities/organisation/tailspin-heavy-lift.md) lift plan for replacing blade B on [WTG-04](../entities/asset/wtg-04.md) at [Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm](../entities/asset/quellmoor-ridge-wind-farm.md). It covers the lift window, responsible persons, crane and rigging data, weather limits, exclusion zone, ground bearing pressure, crane set-up, lift sequence, and sign-off.

## Key facts

- The job is the [WTG-04](../entities/asset/wtg-04.md) [rotor blade](../entities/component/rotor-blade.md) B replacement, described as a single blade lift.[^c1]
- The lift window is from 2026-09-08 to 2026-09-10 and is daylight only.[^c1]
- Bram Okkerse of [Tailspin Heavy Lift](../entities/organisation/tailspin-heavy-lift.md) is the appointed person, and Dario Penwarden of [Contoso Renewables](../entities/organisation/contoso-renewables.md) is the client supervisor.[^c1]
- The main crane is a 600 t crawler crane, fleet number TS-600C-02, with a 102 m main boom.[^c1]
- The assist crane is a 100 t mobile crane used for tailing the blade root.[^c1]
- The blade is 57.5 m long and 14.6 t; the blade yoke is 3.9 t; the rigging is 0.6 t; and the total hook load is 19.1 t.[^c1]
- The lift radius is 26 m, capacity at radius is 31.0 t, utilisation is 62 %, and hook height is 96 m.[^c1]
- The maximum 10-minute mean wind speed at hub height is 8 m/s.[^c1]
- The maximum gust is 11 m/s; no lift is allowed in fog with visibility below 300 m, lightning within 15 km, or icing, and wind is read at the WTG-04 nacelle anemometer and boom tip anemometer.[^c1]
- The exclusion zone radius is 150 m around WTG-04 during the lift.[^c1]
- Two 120 m tag lines are fitted on the blade, and the site gate is closed by the banksman.[^c1]
- Ground bearing pressure under the crane mats is 180 kN/m2, with an allowable value of 250 kN/m2.[^c1]
- Before lifting, the sequence requires a pre-lift meeting and PTW signed by both parties.[^c2]
- The rotor is locked with blade B pointing down and the pitch system isolated.[^c2]
- The blade yoke is fitted and load tested at 0.5 m above the laydown area.[^c2]
- Blade B is unbolted and lowered with both tag lines manned.[^c2]
- The new blade is lifted, bolted, and torqued according to a [Litware](../entities/organisation/litware-wind-systems.md) instruction.[^c2]
- The exclusion zone may be released only by the appointed person.[^c2]
- The plan was prepared by Bram Okkerse as Appointed Person and approved by Dario Penwarden as O&M Supervisor.[^c2]

### Weather limits

The maximum 10-minute mean wind speed at hub height is 8 m/s.[^c1]

The maximum gust is 11 m/s, and lifting is not allowed in fog with visibility below 300 m, with lightning within 15 km, or with icing.[^c1] Wind is read at the [WTG-04](../entities/asset/wtg-04.md) nacelle anemometer and at the boom tip anemometer.[^c1]

### Exclusion zone

The exclusion zone is a 150 m radius around [WTG-04](../entities/asset/wtg-04.md) during the lift.[^c1] Two 120 m tag lines are on the blade, and the site gate is closed by the banksman.[^c1]

### Ground

Ground bearing pressure under the crane mats is stated as 180 kN/m2, with an allowable pressure of 250 kN/m2.[^c1]

### Crane set-up plan view

The plan-view labels show an exclusion zone with R = 150 m, prevailing wind from SW, and a [WTG-04](../entities/asset/wtg-04.md) tower.[^c2]

The same plan-view labels identify an assist crane 100 t, crane TS-600C-02, a radius of R 26 m, a blade laydown area, and a 50 m scale.[^c2]

### Sequence

- Hold the pre-lift meeting and have the PTW signed by both parties.[^c2]
- Lock the rotor with blade B pointing down and isolate the pitch system.[^c2]
- Fit the blade yoke and perform the load test at 0.5 m above the laydown area.[^c2]
- Unbolt and lower blade B with both tag lines manned.[^c2]
- Lift, bolt, and torque the new blade per [Litware](../entities/organisation/litware-wind-systems.md) instruction.[^c2]
- Release the exclusion zone only by the appointed person.[^c2]

### Sign-off

The plan is prepared by Bram Okkerse, Appointed Person, and the adjacent date is 19/08/26.[^c2]

The plan is approved by Dario Penwarden, O&M Supervisor, and the adjacent date is 21/08/26.[^c2]
