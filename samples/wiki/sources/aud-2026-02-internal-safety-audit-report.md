# Internal safety audit report AUD-2026-02

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

This is a [Contoso Renewables](../entities/organisation/contoso-renewables.md) Corporate HSE internal safety audit report for [Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm](../entities/asset/quellmoor-ridge-wind-farm.md). It covers audit scope and method, findings, positive observations, and conclusion with action ownership and due dates.

## Key facts

- The document is [Contoso Renewables](../entities/organisation/contoso-renewables.md) Corporate HSE internal safety audit report AUD-2026-02 for [Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm](../entities/asset/quellmoor-ridge-wind-farm.md).[^c1]
- The audit dates were 2026-02-16 to 2026-02-17, and the report date was 2026-03-06.[^c1]
- The auditor was Ruth Kessington, Corporate HSE Auditor.[^c1]
- The site team comprised Maren Ellingsby as Site Manager, Dario Penwarden as O&M Supervisor, and Greta Vollan as HSE Coordinator.[^c1]
- The audit scope covered the PTW system, lockout/tagout, working at height and rescue, lifting equipment, oil and chemical handling, training records, and emergency arrangements.[^c1]
- The audit method was document review, interviews, and observation of two jobs: a filter change on [WTG-02](../entities/asset/wtg-02.md) and a rescue drill on [WTG-06](../entities/asset/wtg-06.md).[^c1]
- The overall audit score was 86 %, against a target of 85 %.[^c1]
- Finding F1 was a major finding: the [rescue kit](../entities/component/rescue-kit.md) in the WTG-06 nacelle was past its 6-monthly inspection, with last inspection on 2025-06-12 and due date 2025-12-12; the action was to inspect all rescue kits and add the kit seal check to the daily checklist, owned by Greta Vollan and due 2026-03-31.[^c1]
- Finding F2 was a minor finding: two personal padlocks were in use without name tags; the action was to issue tagged padlocks and brief all technicians, owned by Dario Penwarden and due 2026-03-15.[^c1]
- Finding F3 was a minor finding: toolbox talk attendance records were incomplete for November 2025, with one of four sessions missing a sign-in sheet; the action was to reconstruct the records and use a digital sign-in, owned by Greta Vollan and due 2026-04-30.[^c1]
- Finding F4 was an observation: the oil drum storage bund was at 85 % of capacity with 12 drums stored; the action was to reduce the stock in the bund or extend the bund, owned by Tomasz Lebrecht and due 2026-06-30.[^c1]
- Finding F5 was a minor finding: dropped-object exclusion signage was absent at the tower base of [WTG-11](../entities/asset/wtg-11.md) during nacelle work; the action was to provide a portable exclusion signage set for each turbine, owned by Callum Rydings and due 2026-03-31.[^c1]
- Positive observations were that the PTW system was well used, permits were closed correctly, and lockout boards were clear and up to date.[^c1]
- The WTG-06 rescue drill lowered the casualty to the ground in 9 minutes against a 15 minutes target.[^c1]
- The conclusion states that the site meets the corporate standard.[^c1]
- The major finding F1 must be closed before 2026-03-31 and verified by the HSE Coordinator.[^c1]
- The status of all actions is reviewed at the monthly O&M review.[^c1]
