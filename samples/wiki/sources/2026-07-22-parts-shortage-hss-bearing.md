# Parts shortage - HSS bearing FGT-P-22328 below minimum stock

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

This email reports spare-parts shortages at [Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm](../entities/asset/quellmoor-ridge-wind-farm.md), especially the [HSS bearing](../entities/component/hss-bearing.md) [FGT-P-22328](../entities/part/fgt-p-22328.md) being below minimum stock. It describes impacts on [WTG-07](../entities/asset/wtg-07.md) work, delivery timing, related [oil cooler fan motor](../entities/component/cooler-fan-motor.md) stock, and an air-freight option.

## Key facts

- The email was sent by Tomasz Lebrecht to Dario Penwarden on 22 July 2026 with the subject “Parts shortage - [HSS bearing](../entities/component/hss-bearing.md) [FGT-P-22328](../entities/part/fgt-p-22328.md) below minimum stock.”[^c1]
- A stock snapshot dated 21 July showed that HSS bearing FGT-P-22328 on the [generator](../entities/component/generator.md) side was below its minimum level.[^c2]
- For HSS bearing FGT-P-22328, stock was 0 on hand, minimum 1, and 2 on order under PO-76994 to [Fabrikam](../entities/organisation/fabrikam-getriebetechnik.md).[^c3]
- The last HSS bearing was used on [WTG-10](../entities/asset/wtg-10.md) in March under WO-26-0187, and Fabrikam confirmed delivery of two bearings in week 39, from 21 to 25 September 2026.[^c4]
- WO-26-0412 for [WTG-07](../entities/asset/wtg-07.md) HSS bearing replacement cannot start until the bearings arrive.[^c5]
- WTG-07 continues [running](../entities/status-code/s100.md) with vibration above the alarm level, so Yusuf requested weekly vibration reviews.[^c5]
- [Oil cooler fan motor](../entities/component/cooler-fan-motor.md) [LWS-P-30418](../entities/part/lws-p-30418.md) is also below minimum after the WTG-07 repair, with 1 on hand and a minimum of 2.[^c6]
- Two [oil cooler](../entities/component/oil-cooler.md) fan motors are on order under PO-77102 with an expected date of 14 August, and no work order is waiting for that motor.[^c6]
- Fabrikam offered air freight for an extra EUR 2,300, which would bring the bearings forward by about two weeks, and Tomasz asked whether to accept it.[^c7]
- Tomasz Lebrecht signed as Stores and Spare Parts Coordinator for [Contoso Renewables](../entities/organisation/contoso-renewables.md) - [Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm](../entities/asset/quellmoor-ridge-wind-farm.md).[^c8]

## Contents

### Message metadata and status banner

The email was sent by Tomasz Lebrecht to Dario Penwarden, copying Yusuf Arkwell and Maren Ellingsby, on 22 July 2026 with the subject “Parts shortage - [HSS bearing](../entities/component/hss-bearing.md) [FGT-P-22328](../entities/part/fgt-p-22328.md) below minimum stock.”[^c9]

The message contains a “FICTIONAL - NOT FOR OPERATIONAL USE” banner at both the start and end of the body.[^c10][^c11]

### HSS bearing FGT-P-22328 shortage

A 21 July stock snapshot showed that the [HSS bearing](../entities/component/hss-bearing.md) [FGT-P-22328](../entities/part/fgt-p-22328.md) on the [generator](../entities/component/generator.md) side was below its minimum level.[^c12]

The stock status for the bearing was: on hand 0, minimum 1, and on order 2 under PO-76994 to [Fabrikam](../entities/organisation/fabrikam-getriebetechnik.md).[^c13]

The last bearing was used on [WTG-10](../entities/asset/wtg-10.md) in March under WO-26-0187, and Fabrikam confirmed delivery of the two bearings in week 39, from 21 to 25 September 2026.[^c14]

### Operational impact on WTG-07

WO-26-0412 for [WTG-07](../entities/asset/wtg-07.md) [HSS bearing](../entities/component/hss-bearing.md) replacement cannot start until the bearings arrive.[^c15]

WTG-07 keeps [running](../entities/status-code/s100.md) with vibration above the alarm level, and Yusuf asked for weekly vibration reviews.[^c15]

### Oil cooler fan motor LWS-P-30418 stock

The [oil cooler fan motor](../entities/component/cooler-fan-motor.md) [LWS-P-30418](../entities/part/lws-p-30418.md) is also below its minimum after the [WTG-07](../entities/asset/wtg-07.md) repair, with 1 on hand and a minimum of 2.[^c16]

Two motors are on order under PO-77102 with an expected date of 14 August, and no work order is waiting for this motor.[^c16]

### Air-freight option

[Fabrikam](../entities/organisation/fabrikam-getriebetechnik.md) offered air freight for EUR 2,300 extra, which would bring the bearings forward by about two weeks.[^c17]

Tomasz asked whether he should accept the air-freight offer.[^c17]

### Sender signature

Tomasz Lebrecht signed the email as Stores and Spare Parts Coordinator for [Contoso Renewables](../entities/organisation/contoso-renewables.md) - [Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm](../entities/asset/quellmoor-ridge-wind-farm.md).[^c18]
