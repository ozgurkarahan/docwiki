# Shift Handover Note - Day Shift - 18/07/2026

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

This is a French day-shift handover note for [Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm](../entities/asset/quellmoor-ridge-wind-farm.md) dated 18/07/2026. It records turbine maintenance and status observations for [WTG-09](../entities/asset/wtg-09.md), [WTG-07](../entities/asset/wtg-07.md), [WTG-04](../entities/asset/wtg-04.md), and [WTG-12](../entities/asset/wtg-12.md), plus a follow-up action for the next team.

## Key facts

- The note is for [Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm](../entities/asset/quellmoor-ridge-wind-farm.md) and was written by Élise Ternaux, wind turbine technician.[^c1]
- [WTG-09](../entities/asset/wtg-09.md) had its [generator brushes](../entities/component/generator-brushes.md) replaced under work order WO-26-0436 following alarm [A3210](../entities/alarm-code/a3210.md) from 16/07.[^c2]
- For WTG-09, the shortest measured brush was B3 at 16 mm, below the 18 mm threshold in [WI-GEN-014](../entities/procedure/wi-gen-014.md).[^c2]
- For WTG-09, a complete [LWS-P-51207](../entities/part/lws-p-51207.md) set was replaced and the brush-holder screws were tightened to 12 N·m.[^c2]
- WTG-09 was put through run-in at 50 % power for 8 hours, until 20:00 that evening.[^c2]
- [WTG-07](../entities/asset/wtg-07.md) reached a maximum oil temperature of 66 °C that day, had no alarm, and still had high vibration while awaiting the bearing.[^c2]
- [WTG-04](../entities/asset/wtg-04.md) received a ground-based visual inspection of blade B, with nothing new noted.[^c2]
- [WTG-12](../entities/asset/wtg-12.md) had its anti-pollution kit restocked.[^c2]
- The next team was asked to check the end of the WTG-09 run-in at 20:00 and return it to 100 % power.[^c3]

### Shift work and turbine status

- [WTG-09](../entities/asset/wtg-09.md) had its [generator brushes](../entities/component/generator-brushes.md) replaced under work order WO-26-0436 after alarm [A3210](../entities/alarm-code/a3210.md) from 16/07.[^c5]
- The shortest measured brush on WTG-09 was B3 at 16 mm, below the 18 mm threshold in [WI-GEN-014](../entities/procedure/wi-gen-014.md).[^c5]
- A complete [LWS-P-51207](../entities/part/lws-p-51207.md) set was replaced on WTG-09, and the brush-holder screws were tightened to 12 N·m.[^c5]
- WTG-09 run-in was set at 50 % power for 8 hours, until 20:00 that evening.[^c5]
- [WTG-07](../entities/asset/wtg-07.md) had a maximum oil temperature of 66 °C that day, no alarm, and still had high vibration while awaiting the bearing.[^c5]
- [WTG-04](../entities/asset/wtg-04.md) had a ground-based visual inspection of blade B, with nothing new reported.[^c5]
- [WTG-12](../entities/asset/wtg-12.md) had its anti-pollution kit restocked.[^c5]

### Handover action for next team

The next team is to verify the end of [WTG-09](../entities/asset/wtg-09.md) run-in at 20:00 and return it to 100 % power.[^c6] The note repeats that it is fictional and not for operational use.[^c6]
