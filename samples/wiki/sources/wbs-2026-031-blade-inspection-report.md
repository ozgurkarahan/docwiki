# Blade inspection report WBS-2026-031 - Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm (May 2026)

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

This is [Wingtip Blade Services](../entities/organisation/wingtip-blade-services.md)' blade inspection report WBS-2026-031 for [Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm](../entities/asset/quellmoor-ridge-wind-farm.md) in May 2026. It covers a drone visual and ground-photography inspection of 36 blades, with detailed category 3 and category 4 findings and related repair, replacement, monitoring, and follow-up recommendations.

## Key facts

- The report covers [Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm](../entities/asset/quellmoor-ridge-wind-farm.md), which is recorded as having 12 [Litware LT-32](../entities/product/lt-32.md) turbines and 36 blades of 57.5 m.[^c1]
- The inspection dates were 2026-05-18 to 2026-05-22.[^c1]
- The inspection method was drone visual inspection with three passes per blade and ground photography.[^c1]
- The report date was 2026-06-03 and the lead inspector was Leilani Hartfield.[^c1]
- Across the inspection, 36 blades were inspected; there was one category 4 finding, four category 3 findings, and eleven blades with category 2 damage.[^c1]
- Category 3 means significant damage and requires repair within 6 months.[^c1]
- Category 4 means serious damage and requires repair or replacement within 3 months plus monthly drone checks.[^c1]
- Category 5 means critical damage and requires stopping the turbine.[^c1]
- [WTG-04](../entities/asset/wtg-04.md) had the highest overall blade category in the turbine summary: blade A category 2, blade B category 4, blade C category 2, and highest category 4.[^c1]
- Finding F-01 is a category 4 trailing-edge crack on WTG-04 blade B.[^c2]
- For F-01, blade serial LB575-19-1187 had a 0.9 m trailing-edge crack along the bond line between radius 37.6 m and 38.5 m, close to the spar cap.[^c2]
- For F-01, repair is not recommended at that location and WTG-04 blade B is to be replaced before 2026-09-30.[^c2]
- Until WTG-04 blade B is replaced, the crack is to be checked by drone every month and the turbine is to be stopped immediately if the crack exceeds 1.2 m.[^c2]
- Finding F-02 is category 3 leading-edge erosion on WTG-04 blade B, with coating loss between radius 52 m and 57 m and no exposed laminate.[^c2]
- Finding F-03 is category 3 lightning receptor damage on [WTG-08](../entities/asset/wtg-08.md) blade A; the tip receptor is burnt, the receptor is to be replaced within 6 months, and continuity of the down conductor is to be checked.[^c2]
- Finding F-04 is category 3 leading-edge erosion on [WTG-09](../entities/asset/wtg-09.md) blade C, with erosion between radius 50 m and 57 m, exposed laminate over 0.3 m, and a required leading-edge repair within 6 months.[^c2]
- Finding F-05 is a category 3 root gelcoat crack on [WTG-11](../entities/asset/wtg-11.md) blade C, located at radius 3.2 m, with repair within 6 months and a check at the next inspection.[^c2]
- The recommendations are to replace WTG-04 blade B before 2026-09-30 using a crane lift, repair category 3 findings within 6 months using rope access, and perform the next full inspection in May 2027.[^c2]

### Summary and category scale

The inspection covered 36 blades.[^c3] The summary reports one category 4 finding on [WTG-04](../entities/asset/wtg-04.md) blade B, four category 3 findings, and eleven blades with category 2 damage; all other blades were category 0 or 1.[^c3]

The report defines category 0 as no damage with no required action and category 1 as cosmetic with no required action.[^c3] Category 2 is minor damage to be monitored at the next inspection.[^c3] Category 3 is significant damage requiring repair within 6 months.[^c3] Category 4 is serious damage requiring repair or replacement within 3 months and a monthly drone check.[^c3] Category 5 is critical damage requiring the turbine to be stopped.[^c3]

The turbine-level summary records WTG-04 as the only turbine with highest category 4.[^c3] [WTG-08](../entities/asset/wtg-08.md), [WTG-09](../entities/asset/wtg-09.md), and [WTG-11](../entities/asset/wtg-11.md) each have highest category 3.[^c3]

### Detailed findings

- F-01 is a category 4 trailing-edge crack on [WTG-04](../entities/asset/wtg-04.md) blade B.[^c4] The blade serial is LB575-19-1187, and the crack is 0.9 m long along the bond line between radius 37.6 m and 38.5 m, close to the spar cap.[^c4] Repair is not recommended at this location, and blade B is to be replaced before 2026-09-30.[^c4] Until replacement, the crack is to be checked by drone every month, and the turbine is to be stopped immediately if the crack exceeds 1.2 m.[^c4] Figure 1 is the report figure for F-01 and F-02 on WTG-04 blade B.[^c5]
- F-02 is category 3 leading-edge erosion on WTG-04 blade B.[^c4] The report records coating loss between radius 52 m and 57 m, with laminate not exposed.[^c4]
- F-03 is category 3 lightning receptor damage on [WTG-08](../entities/asset/wtg-08.md) blade A.[^c4] The tip receptor is burnt, the receptor is to be replaced within 6 months, and continuity of the down conductor is to be checked.[^c4] Figure 2 is the report figure for F-03 on WTG-08 blade A.[^c5]
- F-04 is category 3 leading-edge erosion on [WTG-09](../entities/asset/wtg-09.md) blade C.[^c4] The erosion is between radius 50 m and 57 m, laminate is exposed over 0.3 m, and leading-edge repair is required within 6 months.[^c4]
- F-05 is a category 3 root gelcoat crack on [WTG-11](../entities/asset/wtg-11.md) blade C.[^c4] The crack is at radius 3.2 m, with repair required within 6 months and a check at the next inspection.[^c4]

### Recommendations and preparation

The recommendations are to replace [WTG-04](../entities/asset/wtg-04.md) blade B before 2026-09-30, with a crane lift required.[^c4] The category 3 findings are to be repaired within 6 months using rope access.[^c4] The next full inspection is listed as May 2027.[^c4]

The report was prepared by Leilani Hartfield, Lead Blade Inspector, [Wingtip Blade Services](../entities/organisation/wingtip-blade-services.md).[^c4]
