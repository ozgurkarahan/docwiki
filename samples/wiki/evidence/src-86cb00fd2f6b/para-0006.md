## Text

> Between 2026-04-09 and 2026-06-21 WTG-07 logged 27 A2101 warnings (gearbox oil sump temperature high, 75 °C) and two A2102 trips (82 °C): on 2026-05-30 at 14:12 (82.6 °C) and on 2026-06-19 at 15:47 (83.4 °C). No other turbine logged an A2101 warning in the same period.
