# MOC-2025-012 - Gearbox Oil Grade Change to PWG 220 — ¶ 4

Source page: [MOC-2025-012 - Gearbox Oil Grade Change to PWG 220](../../sources/moc-2025-012-gearbox-oil-grade-change.md) · Original file: [original.docx](original.docx) · Provenance: ✓ quote verified (native text)

## Text

> Replace Proseware PWG 320 (ISO VG 320) with Proseware PWG 220 (ISO VG 220) in the gearboxes of all 12 turbines, as required by service bulletin SB-LWS-2025-07 Rev. A. Litware support confirmed on 2025-05-20 (ticket LWS-TS-48213) that the bulletin applies to all 12 CCV turbines of Quellmoor Ridge.
