# SOP-WAH-003 Revision B - Working at height and rescue — ¶ 12

Source page: [SOP-WAH-003 Revision B - Working at height and rescue](../../sources/sop-wah-003-revb-working-at-height-and-rescue.md) · Original file: [original.docx](original.docx) · Provenance: ✓ quote verified (native text)

## Text

> Tool tethering is mandatory above 2 m. Hatches stay closed while tools or parts are passed. During nacelle work an exclusion zone with signage is set up at the tower base and nobody enters it without the agreement of the nacelle team.
