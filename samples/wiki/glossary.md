# Glossary

Terms of the domain with definitions, synonyms in other languages and related pages.

## bearing

Machine element that supports a rotating shaft.

- Synonyms: fr: roulement · de: Lager
- See also: [Gearbox temperature, vibration and oil analysis](topics/gearbox-condition-monitoring.md)

## curtailment

Deliberate reduction or stop of power production, for example because of permit conditions or a grid limit.

- Synonyms: fr: bridage · de: Abregelung, Abschaltung
- See also: [Environmental permit and night curtailment](topics/environmental-compliance.md), [Grid connection and grid operator instructions](topics/grid-operations.md)

## delivery date

Date on which a supplier delivers ordered parts.

- Synonyms: fr: date de livraison · de: Liefertermin
- See also: [Spare parts, stock levels and supply](topics/spare-parts-and-supply.md)

## environmental permit

Authorisation issued by the environmental regulator with conditions on the operation of the site.

- Synonyms: fr: autorisation environnementale · de: Umweltgenehmigung
- See also: [Environmental permit and night curtailment](topics/environmental-compliance.md)

## exclusion zone

Area that nobody enters during work above it or during a lift.

- Synonyms: fr: zone d'exclusion · de: Sperrbereich
- See also: [Safety, isolation and work at height](topics/safety-and-access.md)

## gear oil

Lubricant of the gearbox; the manufacturer specifies its product and viscosity grade.

- Synonyms: fr: huile de multiplicateur, huile d'engrenage · de: Getriebeöl
- See also: [Gearbox lubrication and oil changes](topics/gearbox-lubrication.md)

## gearbox

Mechanical transmission between the rotor shaft and the generator that raises the rotational speed.

- Synonyms: fr: multiplicateur, boîte de vitesses · de: Getriebe
- See also: [gearbox](entities/component/gearbox.md), [Gearbox lubrication and oil changes](topics/gearbox-lubrication.md)

## generator brush

Carbon brush that carries current to the generator rotor through the slip ring.

- Synonyms: fr: balai de génératrice, balai · de: Generatorbürste, Kohlebürste
- See also: [generator brushes](entities/component/generator-brushes.md), [Generator brushes and electrical systems](topics/generator-and-electrical.md)

## leading-edge erosion

Wear of the front edge of a blade by rain, hail and particles.

- Synonyms: fr: érosion du bord d'attaque · de: Vorderkantenerosion
- See also: [Blades, pitch bearings and main-component lifts](topics/blades-and-rotor.md)

## lift plan

Plan of a crane lift with loads, radius, weather limits and exclusion zone.

- Synonyms: fr: plan de levage · de: Hebeplan
- See also: [Blades, pitch bearings and main-component lifts](topics/blades-and-rotor.md)

## lockout/tagout

Isolation of all energy sources with personal padlocks and tags before work starts.

- Synonyms: fr: consignation · de: Freischaltung
- See also: [SOP-LOTO-001 Lockout/tagout of wind turbines](entities/procedure/sop-loto-001.md), [Safety, isolation and work at height](topics/safety-and-access.md)

## management of change

Formal review and approval of a technical or organisational change before it is made.

- Synonyms: fr: gestion des modifications · de: Änderungsmanagement
- See also: [Gearbox lubrication and oil changes](topics/gearbox-lubrication.md)

## minimum stock level

Quantity below which a part must be reordered.

- Synonyms: fr: stock minimum · de: Mindestbestand
- See also: [Spare parts, stock levels and supply](topics/spare-parts-and-supply.md)

## near miss

Unplanned event that could have caused harm but did not.

- Synonyms: fr: presque accident · de: Beinaheunfall
- See also: [Safety, isolation and work at height](topics/safety-and-access.md)

## oil analysis

Laboratory analysis of an oil sample (wear metals, water, viscosity, particles).

- Synonyms: fr: analyse d'huile · de: Ölanalyse
- See also: [Gearbox temperature, vibration and oil analysis](topics/gearbox-condition-monitoring.md)

## oil cooler

Air/oil heat exchanger with a fan that removes heat from the gearbox oil.

- Synonyms: fr: refroidisseur d'huile · de: Ölkühler
- See also: [oil cooler](entities/component/oil-cooler.md), [Gearbox temperature, vibration and oil analysis](topics/gearbox-condition-monitoring.md)

## oil filter element

Replaceable filter cartridge of the gearbox oil circuit.

- Synonyms: fr: élément filtrant · de: Ölfilterelement
- See also: [oil filter element](entities/component/oil-filter.md)

## operating hours

Time during which a machine has been running.

- Synonyms: fr: heures de fonctionnement · de: Betriebsstunden
- See also: [Gearbox lubrication and oil changes](topics/gearbox-lubrication.md)

## pitch bearing

Bearing that connects a blade to the hub and lets the blade turn about its axis.

- Synonyms: fr: roulement de pale · de: Blattlager
- See also: [pitch bearing](entities/component/pitch-bearing.md), [Blades, pitch bearings and main-component lifts](topics/blades-and-rotor.md)

## rescue kit

Sealed kit with a descent device used to lower a casualty from the nacelle.

- Synonyms: fr: kit de sauvetage · de: Rettungsset
- See also: [rescue kit](entities/component/rescue-kit.md)

## rotor blade

One of the three blades of the rotor.

- Synonyms: fr: pale · de: Rotorblatt
- See also: [rotor blade](entities/component/rotor-blade.md), [Blades, pitch bearings and main-component lifts](topics/blades-and-rotor.md)

## run-in

Period of reduced load after new parts are fitted.

- Synonyms: fr: rodage · de: Einlaufen
- See also: [Generator brushes and electrical systems](topics/generator-and-electrical.md)

## SCADA

Supervisory control and data acquisition system that records turbine alarms and status changes.

- Synonyms: fr: SCADA · de: SCADA
- See also: [Gearbox temperature, vibration and oil analysis](topics/gearbox-condition-monitoring.md)

## service bulletin

Instruction from the turbine manufacturer that changes maintenance requirements or parts.

- Synonyms: fr: bulletin de service · de: Service-Bulletin
- See also: [Gearbox lubrication and oil changes](topics/gearbox-lubrication.md)

## shift handover

Note passed from one shift to the next with the status of turbines and open work.

- Synonyms: fr: note de relève · de: Schichtübergabe
- See also: [Training, site rules and acronyms](topics/training-and-onboarding.md)

## slip ring

Rotating ring on the generator shaft on which the brushes slide.

- Synonyms: fr: bague collectrice · de: Schleifring
- See also: [slip ring](entities/component/slip-ring.md)

## spare part

Part kept in stock to repair or maintain the turbines.

- Synonyms: fr: pièce de rechange · de: Ersatzteil
- See also: [Spare parts, stock levels and supply](topics/spare-parts-and-supply.md)

## toolbox talk

Short safety briefing held with the team before work.

- Synonyms: fr: causerie sécurité · de: Sicherheitsunterweisung
- See also: [Training, site rules and acronyms](topics/training-and-onboarding.md)

## vibration velocity

Vibration level expressed as the RMS value of the velocity, in mm/s.

- Synonyms: fr: vitesse vibratoire · de: Schwinggeschwindigkeit
- See also: [Gearbox temperature, vibration and oil analysis](topics/gearbox-condition-monitoring.md)

## viscosity grade

Classification of an industrial oil by its kinematic viscosity at 40 °C (ISO VG).

- Synonyms: fr: grade de viscosité · de: Viskositätsklasse
- See also: [Gearbox lubrication and oil changes](topics/gearbox-lubrication.md)

## work order

Record of a maintenance job with its turbine, parts, dates and status.

- Synonyms: fr: ordre de travail, OT · de: Arbeitsauftrag
- See also: [Spare parts, stock levels and supply](topics/spare-parts-and-supply.md)

## working at height

Work where a person could fall, such as climbing a tower or working in the nacelle.

- Synonyms: fr: travail en hauteur · de: Arbeiten in der Höhe
- See also: [SOP-WAH-003 Working at height and rescue](entities/procedure/sop-wah-003.md), [Safety, isolation and work at height](topics/safety-and-access.md)
