# Quellmoor Ridge O&M wiki

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Contoso Renewables, Quellmoor Ridge Wind Farm and every organisation, person, product and value in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

Operations and maintenance of an onshore wind farm: OEM manuals and service bulletins, procedures and work instructions, incident and change records, maintenance, stock and SCADA data, laboratory and inspection reports, permits and grid instructions, training decks, correspondence, field notes and meeting records.

Every fact in this wiki is cited: footnotes link to evidence sidecars that show the exact page, paragraph, slide or table rows of the original document.

## How to navigate

1. Start with the [topics](topics/index.md) for syntheses across documents.
2. Browse the [entities](entities/index.md) of the domain registry.
3. Read the [sources](sources/index.md): one page per document, grouped by category, with its status and sensitivity.
4. Use the [glossary](glossary.md) for acronyms and multilingual synonyms.
5. Check the [log](log.md) for recent changes and the [quality report](_meta/quality.md) for gate results.

Agents start with the [agent guide](AGENTS.md); `llms.txt` and `catalog.json` index every page.
