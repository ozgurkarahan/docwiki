# Which gearbox oil grade applies to WTG-07 in winter?

> FICTIONAL - NOT FOR OPERATIONAL USE. Sample content about the fictional operator Contoso Renewables.

ISO VG 220. The current procedure requires ISO VG 220 gearbox oil at cold sites and names WTG-07 as the first turbine to switch.[^c1] The 2026 maintenance plan still lists ISO VG 320 for the 2026-03 oil change on WTG-07, which contradicts the procedure; the procedure has the higher authority.[^c2]
