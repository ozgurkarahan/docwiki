# Gearbox oil change SOP rev A

> FICTIONAL - NOT FOR OPERATIONAL USE. Sample content about the fictional operator Contoso Renewables.

> **Superseded** by [Gearbox oil change SOP rev B](sop-gbx-010-rev-b.md). Use the current revision.

Revision A of SOP-GBX-010 described the gearbox oil change for Litware gearboxes with a single oil grade for every turbine.

## Key facts

- Revision A specifies ISO VG 320 gearbox oil for all turbines.[^c1]
- The oil is drained while it is still warm.[^c2]

## Contents

### Oil grade

Use ISO VG 320 gearbox oil for all turbines.[^c1]

### Draining

Drain the oil while it is still warm.[^c2]
