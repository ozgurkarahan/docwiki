# Gearbox oil change SOP rev B

> FICTIONAL - NOT FOR OPERATIONAL USE. Sample content about the fictional operator Contoso Renewables.

Revision B of SOP-GBX-010 is the current gearbox oil change procedure for Litware gearboxes. It adds a dedicated oil grade for cold sites and supersedes [revision A](sop-gbx-010-rev-a.md).

## Key facts

- At cold sites, where the ambient temperature can fall below -20 °C, ISO VG 220 gearbox oil is used.[^c1]
- WTG-07 is the first turbine to switch to ISO VG 220.[^c1]
- Revision B supersedes SOP-GBX-010 rev A.[^c2]

## Contents

### Cold sites

Cold sites can fall below -20 °C and use ISO VG 220 gearbox oil; [WTG-07](../entities/asset/wtg-07.md) switches first.[^c1]

### Supersession

This revision replaces SOP-GBX-010 rev A for Litware gearboxes.[^c2]
