# Glossary

## ISO VG

ISO viscosity grade of an industrial oil, for example ISO VG 220.

- Synonyms: fr: grade de viscosité ISO · de: ISO-Viskositätsklasse
- See also: [Gearbox oil](topics/gearbox-oil.md)

## SOP

Standard operating procedure.

- Synonyms: fr: procédure opératoire standard · de: Standardarbeitsanweisung
- See also: [Gearbox oil change SOP rev B](sources/sop-gbx-010-rev-b.md)

## WTG

Wind turbine generator; turbines are identified as WTG-NN.

- Synonyms: en: wind turbine generator · fr: éolienne · de: Windenergieanlage
- See also: [WTG-07](entities/asset/wtg-07.md)
