# Glossary

Terms of the domain with definitions, synonyms in other languages and related pages.

## revision

A new issue of the same numbered document (quote or schedule), identified by a letter; a later revision supersedes the earlier ones.

- Synonyms: fr: indice · en: revision letter
- See also: [Schedule and window delivery](topics/schedule-and-window-delivery.md)

## schedule

The planned sequence of the tasks of a job, with their dates, the trade doing them and their progress.

- Synonyms: fr: planning, planning de travaux, calendrier des travaux · en: works schedule, programme
- See also: [Schedule and window delivery](topics/schedule-and-window-delivery.md)

## windows and external doors

The windows, glazed doors and sliding bay windows of a building, and the trade that supplies and installs them.

- Synonyms: fr: menuiseries extérieures, menuiseries · en: exterior joinery, joinery
- See also: [Lot 5 - Menuiseries extérieures](entities/work-package/lot-5-menuiseries-exterieures.md), [Schedule and window delivery](topics/schedule-and-window-delivery.md)
