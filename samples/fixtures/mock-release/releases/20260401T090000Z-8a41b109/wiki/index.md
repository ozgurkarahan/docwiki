# Chantier Moreau mock wiki

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Lamna Bâtiment, its chantiers (the Moreau household, the résidence Les Nacrines, the école de Montferrine), Valbrise, Montferrine and every organisation, person, product, amount and date in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

This wiki compiles three fictional documents of the Chantier Moreau job file of the general contractor Lamna Bâtiment: two revisions of the schedule and the minutes of a site meeting. The documents are in French. Every fact links to an evidence sidecar that shows the exact page, paragraph or sheet rows it came from.
