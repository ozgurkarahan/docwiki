# Lot 5 - Menuiseries extérieures

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Lamna Bâtiment, its chantiers (the Moreau household, the résidence Les Nacrines, the école de Montferrine), Valbrise, Montferrine and every organisation, person, product, amount and date in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

Work package 5 ("lot 5"), the windows ("menuiseries extérieures") of the extension: the schedule follows their delivery in task T07 and their installation in task T08.

## Facts

- Task T07, the delivery of the windows by [VanArsdel Menuiseries](../organisation/vanarsdel-menuiseries.md), is planned on 2026-04-20 in indice B of the schedule.[^c1]
- Task T08, the installation of the windows, is planned from 2026-04-21 to 2026-04-23.[^c1]
- The delivery was first planned on 2026-03-30 in indice A, now superseded.[^c2]
- The minutes of site meeting n°3 record the postponement from 2026-03-30 to 2026-04-20.[^c3]

## Timeline

- 2026-03-18: [VanArsdel Menuiseries](../organisation/vanarsdel-menuiseries.md) announces the postponement of the delivery by e-mail.[^c3]
- 2026-03-30: first planned delivery date, in indice A of the schedule (superseded).[^c2]
- 2026-04-20: delivery of the windows (task T07).[^c1]
- 2026-04-21 to 2026-04-23: installation of the windows (task T08).[^c1]
