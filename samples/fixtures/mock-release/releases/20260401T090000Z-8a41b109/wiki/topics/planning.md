# Planning

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Lamna Bâtiment, its chantiers (the Moreau household, the résidence Les Nacrines, the école de Montferrine), Valbrise, Montferrine and every organisation, person, product, amount and date in this wiki are invented. Not for operational use.

Delivery and installation of the windows of the extension and the progress of the masonry, compiled from three sources.

## Overview

The windows of the extension (task T07, "Livraison des menuiseries") are delivered on 2026-04-20 and installed from 2026-04-21 to 2026-04-23 (task T08, "Pose des menuiseries").[^c1] The window maker announced the postponement by e-mail on 2026-03-18 and the minutes of site meeting n°3 record it.[^c2]

## Window delivery

- Indice B of the schedule gives the delivery of the windows on 2026-04-20, with the remark "Livraison reportée".[^c1]
- The minutes of site meeting n°3 report the postponement from 2026-03-30 to 2026-04-20 for the order BC-2026-009.[^c2]

## Masonry progress

- The walls ("élévation des murs", task T05) are at 100 % in indice B of the schedule.[^c1]
- The minutes of site meeting n°3 record the walls at 70 %.[^c3]

## Contradictions

- **Masonry walls progress.** [Planning and purchase tracking, indice B](../sources/pln-2026-003-indice-b-planning-et-suivi-des-achats.md) states: The walls (task T05) are at 100 %.[^c1] (authority 4, dated 2026-03-27). [Site meeting minutes n°3](../sources/2026-03-24-compte-rendu-de-chantier-n3.md) states: The walls are at 70 %.[^c3] (authority 7, dated 2026-03-24). Resolution rule (authority, then recency): Site meeting minutes n°3 prevails (authority 7 vs 4).

Resolution policy: signed site meeting minutes (authority 7) prevail over the schedule (authority 4); a later revision (indice) of the schedule supersedes the earlier one.

## Superseded information

- **Window delivery date.** [Planning and purchase tracking, indice A](../sources/pln-2026-003-indice-a-planning-et-suivi-des-achats.md) stated: The delivery of the windows (task T07) on 2026-03-30 and their installation (task T08) from 2026-03-31 to 2026-04-02.[^c4] Replacement: The delivery of the windows on 2026-04-20 and their installation from 2026-04-21 to 2026-04-23, after the postponement announced on 2026-03-18.[^c1][^c5] Superseding source: [Planning and purchase tracking, indice B](../sources/pln-2026-003-indice-b-planning-et-suivi-des-achats.md).
