Indice A of the schedule ("planning") PLN-2026-003, dated 2026-03-17 and shown here as a printed PDF page. It planned the delivery of the windows on 2026-03-30.

## Key facts

- Indice A of the schedule PLN-2026-003 is dated 2026-03-17.[^c1]
- Indice A plans the delivery of the windows (task T07) on 2026-03-30.[^c1]
- Indice A plans the installation of the windows (task T08) from 2026-03-31 to 2026-04-02.[^c1]
- Indice A shows the walls ("élévation des murs", task T05) at 20 %.[^c1]

## Contents

Task T07 ("Livraison des menuiseries") is carried out by [VanArsdel Menuiseries](../entities/organisation/vanarsdel-menuiseries.md) and refers to the order BC-2026-009; task T08 ("Pose des menuiseries") follows the next day.[^c1]
